# Muscle Power

## Learning Objectives

To develop physical strength and resilience through disciplined training; fostering brotherhood and sportsmanship through healthy competition; understanding the concept of struggle (Jihad al-Nafs) by pushing physical limits; and building the confidence required for societal leadership.

## Activity 1: Muscle Power
