# Writing Activities Set 1

> [!info] Learning Objectives
> Critical thinking from an Islamic paradigm · Articulating a robust Islamic worldview · Persuasive writing & public speaking · Deconstructing secular narratives · Strengthening Islamic identity · Fostering intellectual brotherhood (*Ukhuwwah*).

## Activity 1: Counter-Narrative

> [!note] Setup & Atmosphere
> - **Topic Cards:** Murabbi prepares 5–10 cards featuring secular or anti-Islamic tropes (e.g. *"Religion is purely private"*, *"Follow your desires for freedom"*).
> - **Submission Language:** Urdu
> - **Time Limit:** 5 mins intro, 15 mins writing (100–150 words), 20–30 mins recitation & evaluation.

```text
=====================================================
    SHABAB COUNTER-NARRATIVE SUBMISSION TEMPLATE
=====================================================
Topic Prompt: "Religion is a private personal matter"
Author: [Shabab Name] | Group: [Ameer Name]

Core Refutation (Haqq):
[100-150 words concise refutation demonstrating how
Islam is a complete Deen & way of life for society]
=====================================================
```

> [!success] Evaluation Rubric (20 Points)
> - **Aqeedah & Conviction (/5):** Correctness & strength of Islamic worldview.
> - **Clarity & Impact (/5):** Eloquence and persuasiveness (*Bayan*).
> - **Use of Evidence (/5):** Reasoning & textual evidence (*Daleel*).
> - **Adherence to Prompt (/5):** Direct refutation of the card's argument.

## Activity 2: A Legacy of the Lions

> [!note] Reflective Exercise
> An exercise in self-accountability before taking account on Qiyamah (*Muhasabah*).

> [!quote] Choose One Prompt Path (Urdu Submission)
> - **Prompt A: The Final Verdict:** Write the eulogy you hope a God-fearing friend can give after your Janazah. What character, deeds, and impact will he recount?
> - **Prompt B: Letter to My Son:** Write a 1-page letter to your 15-year-old future son outlining the essential principles he must live by as a Muslim man.

> [!success] Evaluation Rubric (20 Points)
> - **Islamic Aspiration (/5):** Prioritizing Deen over worldly materialism.
> - **Sincerity & Depth (/5):** Sincere self-reflection (*Ikhlas*).
> - **Clarity of Legacy (/5):** Clear articulation of values and impact.
> - **Adherence to Prompt (/5):** Fulfilling prompt requirements.
