# Boni Kushti

## How to Play

**Inspired by:** One legged Kushti

- **Age Limit:** 14+
- **Group Size:** 5–10 players
- **Duration:** 30 minutes
- **Prep Time:** < 5 minutes
- **Difficulty:** 4
- **Code:** O-420

### Reference

Watch: https://m.youtube.com/watch?v=ZqfeLN8VMNE
