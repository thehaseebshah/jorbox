# Adrenaline Arena

## Learning Objectives

Physical fitness, Motor skills development, Hand-eye coordination, Spatial awareness, Strategic thinking, Problem-solving, Teamwork, Communication, Discipline, Resilience, Self-confidence, Sportsmanship.

## General Instructions

Choose only one of the following activities. But if time permits and Shabab get bored, you can also conduct the other one. We include multiple activities for three reasons: A/B Testing, adding variation to counter potential boredom, and to fill out the gap for the skills lesson if an activity is completed very early.

## Activity 1: Rugby

### Game Setup

- **Teams:** 2 teams, each consisting of 6 or more players.
- **Field:** Use chalk, sticks, or tape to mark:
  - A halfway line (center of the field)
  - Two scoring lines (one at each end of the field)
  - Two cones or bags on each end to mark the scoring zones
- **Ball:** Use any football (no need for a rugby ball).

### How to Play

- **Objective:** The goal is to get the ball past the opponent's scoring line and place it on the ground in the scoring zone to earn 5 points.
- **Starting Play:** A coin toss decides the starting team. The game begins from the halfway line.
- **Moving the Ball:** Players can run with the ball or pass it backward/sideways only (no forward passes). Kicking is optional, but it should be safe and low. (You can try two variations – one with kicking allowed and one with not allowed.)
- **Tackling:** A tackle occurs when a player grabs the player with the ball to stop their movement or gently bring them to the ground. Multiple players can tackle together. The tackled player must immediately drop the ball.
- **After a Tackle:** Any player from the opposing team can pick up the ball and continue play.
- **Time Limit Rule:** A player can hold the ball for a maximum of 5 seconds before passing.
- **Out of Bounds:** If the ball goes out of bounds, restart with a throw-in from the point where the ball went out.
- **Game Time:** The game consists of two halves, each lasting 10 minutes, with a short break in between.

### Scoring

To score 5 points, place the ball on the ground in the opponent's scoring zone.

### Safety First

- Teach safe, controlled tackling (start gently).
- No rough play.
- Emphasize teamwork, passing, and fun.

## Activity 2: Handball

### Game Setup

- **Teams:** 2 teams, each consisting of 6 players (or adjust based on your group size). You can have multiple groups playing if you have more than one football.
- **Field:** Mark a rectangle (~20x10 meters) using chalk, cones, or sticks, or bags (backpacks). Include:
  - A halfway line
  - Two goal areas, each 2 to 3 meters wide, marked with cones or bags
  - Around each goal, mark a 3-step square "no-entry zone" (similar to a goalie box). Only the goalkeeper is allowed inside this zone.
- **Ball:** Use any football or handball-sized ball. A ball smaller than a football would be preferred more, if you have one.

### How to Play

- **Objective:** Score by throwing the ball into the opponent's goal (between the cones). Each goal = 1 point.
- **Starting Play:** A coin toss decides the starting team. The game begins from the halfway line.
- **Ball Movement:** Players can dribble (bounce) the ball freely — no limit. You can take up to 3 steps while holding the ball, both before and after dribbling. After 3 steps: you must pass, shoot, or dribble.
- **Goal Area Rule ("No-Entry Zone"):** Around each goal is a 3-step square box (similar to a goalkeeper area). Only the goalkeeper can enter this zone. Shooters must throw from outside the box — no jumping inside or landing in it!
- **Defence Rules:** Defenders can block shots and passes using their hands and body. No pushing, hitting, tripping, or aggressive contact. Light shoulder-to-shoulder contact is allowed.
- **Shooting Rules:** Shots must be taken from outside the no-entry zone. The ball must be thrown below shoulder height when entering the goal area. A ball above shoulder height will not be a goal.
- **Time Rule:** A player can hold the ball for up to 3 seconds without dribbling.
- **Out of Bounds:** If the ball goes out, the opposing team restarts with a throw-in from that spot.
- **Game Time:** Play 2 halves of 10 minutes each, with a short break in between.

### Safety Tips

- Teach safe contact and smart defense.
- No shoving or wild tackles — it's handball, not parkour wrestling.
- Rotate goalies often so everyone gets a chance.
