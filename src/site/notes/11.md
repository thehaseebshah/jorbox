# Adrenaline Arena

> [!info] Learning Objectives
> Physical fitness · Motor skills development · Hand-eye coordination · Spatial awareness · Strategic thinking · Problem-solving · Teamwork · Communication · Discipline · Resilience · Self-confidence · Sportsmanship.

> [!note] General Instructions for Murabbi
> Choose only one of the following activities. If time permits and Shabab want variation, conduct the second one.

## Activity 1: Rugby

> [!note] Game Setup
> - **Teams:** 2 teams of 6+ players.
> - **Field:** Mark halfway line, 2 end scoring lines, and 2 scoring cones per side.
> - **Ball:** Standard football.

### How to Play

- **Objective:** Place the ball in the opponent's scoring zone for **5 points**.
- **Movement:** Run or pass backward/sideways only (no forward passes). Kicking is optional.
- **Tackling:** Gently grab the ball-carrier to stop them. Tackled player must drop the ball immediately.
- **5-Second Rule:** Max 5 seconds holding the ball before passing.
- **Time:** Two 10-minute halves.

> [!warning] Safety First
> Teach safe, controlled tackling (start gently). No rough play. Emphasize passing, teamwork, and sportsmanship.

## Activity 2: Handball

> [!note] Game Setup
> - **Teams:** 2 teams of 6 players.
> - **Field:** 20×10m rectangle with halfway line and 2–3m goal areas with a 3-step "no-entry zone" around each goal.

### How to Play

- **Objective:** Throw the ball into opponent's goal (**1 point**).
- **Movement:** Dribble freely. Take up to 3 steps holding the ball. Max 3-second hold.
- **No-Entry Zone:** Only the goalkeeper can enter the 3-step box. Shooters must throw from outside!
- **Shooting Rule:** Throws must enter below shoulder height.

> [!tip] Safety Tips
> No wild tackles or shoving. Rotate goalies often so everyone gets a turn.
