# Scavenger Hunt

> [!info] Learning Objectives
> Observation skills & attention to detail · Strategic planning & time management · Team coordination · Problem-solving under pressure · Physical activity & spatial awareness · Resourcefulness · Precision in instructions (*Ittiba'*) · Building trust (*Amanah*) · Strengthening brotherhood (*Ukhuwwah*).

## Activity 1: Scavenger Hunt

Watch this video guide: [16 Scavenger Hunt](https://www.canva.com/design/DAGtdI1qbao/gLhdIQ2W-2mAG0AS8kueVw/watch).

There are different ways to reveal location and give challenges:

- Crossword Puzzle
- Bingo
- Intelligence Challenge
- Optical Illusions Challenge
- Other Challenges
