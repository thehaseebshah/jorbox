# Writing Activities Set 1

> [!info] Learning Objectives
> Critical thinking from an Islamic paradigm, Articulating a robust Islamic worldview, Persuasive writing & public speaking, Deconstructing secular narratives, Strengthening Islamic identity, Fostering intellectual brotherhood (*Ukhuwwah*).

## General Instructions

## Activity 1: Counter-Narrative

### Setup

- **Prepare the Gauntlet:** The Murabbi prepares 5-10 topic cards with common anti-Islamic or secular arguments.
- **Arrange the Majlis:** Shabab sit in a semi-circle, ready to write.

This is an Essay Writing Activity.

### How to Participate

1. **The Spark (5 mins):** The Murabbi gives a brief introduction about intellectual warfare and defending the truth.
2. **Draw Your Topic (1 min):** Each participant draws a topic card.
3. **Forge the Argument (15 mins):** Each participant writes a sharp, concise refutation (100-150 words).
4. **Unleash the Kalam (20-30 mins):** Each participant stands and reads their counter-narrative aloud.

**Language of Submission:** Urdu

### Evaluation & Scoring

The Murabbi will score each piece out of 20 points based on:

| Criterion | Description | Points |
|
---|
---|
---|
| Aqeedah & Conviction | How well does the argument reflect a strong Islamic worldview? | / 5 |
| Clarity & Impact (Bayan) | How clearly and persuasively is the idea communicated? | / 5 |
| Use of Evidence (Daleel) | How effectively are Islamic principles or reasoning used? | / 5 |
| Adherence to Prompt | How well does the response directly address the topic? | / 5 |
| **Total Score** | | **/ 20** |

## Activity 2: A Legacy of the Lions

### Setup

- **Present the Prompts:** The Murabbi presents prompt options clearly.
- **Set the Atmosphere:** Ensure a quiet, serious, and reflective environment.

### How to Participate

1. **The Reminder (5 mins):** Short talk on legacy in Islam vs. materialism.
2. **Choose Your Path (1 min):** Select one of the two prompts.
3. **The Ink Flows (25 mins):** Participants write focusing on sincerity (*Ikhlas*).
4. **The Circle of Brothers (10-15 mins):** Share responses and collect for scoring.

**Language of Submission:** Urdu

### Prompt Options

**Prompt A: The Final Verdict:** Write the eulogy you hope a close, God-fearing friend would be able to give after your Janazah. What character, deeds, and impact would he speak of?

**Prompt B: Letter to My Son:** Write a one-page letter to your 15-year-old future son, outlining the most important principles he must live by as a Muslim man.
