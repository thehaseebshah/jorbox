# Home Golf

## How to Play
**How to Play:** Design a golf course at home and play with friends, count how many hits it takes to reach each hole by each person.

- **Age Limit:** 10+
- **Group Size:** 2+
- **Duration:** 30 minutes
- **Prep Time:** < 5 minutes
- **Difficulty:** Easy
- **Rating:** 5

### Items Needed

- Stick
- Ball
