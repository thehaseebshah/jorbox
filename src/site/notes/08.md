## Activity 1: Natural Direction Finding

Navigation is a critical survival skill fostering self-reliance, confidence, and resilience against technology failures.

> [!tip] 1. Celestial Navigation
> **Using the Sun:**
> - *Solar Noon:* Sun at highest point indicates South in Northern Hemisphere (North in Southern).
> - *Shadow Method:* Place stick upright, mark shadow tip. Wait 15–30 mins, mark new tip. A line drawn from 1st to 2nd mark points West → East.
> - *Watch Method:* Point hour hand at sun. Line bisecting angle between hour hand and 12 o'clock points South.
>
> **Using the Stars & Planets at Night:**
> - *Polaris (North Star):* Located directly above North Pole. Find Big Dipper outer bowl stars; draw imaginary line straight to Polaris.
> - *Moon & Planets:* Illuminated crescent tips point roughly West.

> [!note] 2. Natural Environment Markers
> Moss often grows more abundantly on the north side of tree trunks in the Northern Hemisphere. Observe animal paths, river flow directions, and prevailing wind patterns.

## Activity 2: Solar Oven Challenge

> [!example] Instructions for Building
> 1. **Prepare Box:** Pizza box lid flap recommended. Paint interior black or line with black paper.
> 2. **Reflective Surface:** Cover inside lid with smooth aluminum foil.
> 3. **Clear Cover:** Seal opening with clear plastic wrap/oven bag to trap heat (greenhouse effect).
> 4. **Assemble & Adjust:** Prop reflective lid at an angle using a stick to maximize sunlight intake. Test by melting marshmallows or cooking small vegetables!

## Activity 3: Water Filtration

> [!important] 4-Layer Natural Filtration System
> 1. **Setup:** Cut plastic bottle bottom for funnel; insert upside down into intact second bottle.
> 2. **Base Filter:** Place coffee filter/fine cloth at neck.
> 3. **Layering (Bottom to Top):**
>    - Gravel / Small stones (filters large debris)
>    - Sand (filters fine particles)
>    - Activated charcoal (absorbs chemical impurities)
>    - Top Sand layer
> 4. **Drip Process:** Slowly pour dirty water into top and observe clear water collection below.
> 
> *Tip: Can also be built using a single bottle with tissue paper at the spout.*

## Activity 4: Igniting Fire with Natural Methods

> [!warning] Safety & Fire Regulations
> Always prioritize safety and local regulations. Requires patience (*Sabr*) and reliance on effort.

> [!important] The 3 Required Fuel Tiers
> - **Tinder:** Highly flammable (dry leaves, fine bark shavings, dry grass).
> - **Kindling:** Small twigs/sticks catching fire from tinder.
> - **Fuel:** Larger wood logs to sustain the fire.

> [!tip] Fire-Starting Techniques
> - **Friction:** Bow drill, hand drill, or fire plough (rubbing wood to generate glowing ember).
> - **Flint & Steel:** Striking steel against sharp flint to catch sparks on char cloth/tinder.
> - **Solar Lens:** Using magnifying glass or clear ice to focus sunbeams onto tinder.
