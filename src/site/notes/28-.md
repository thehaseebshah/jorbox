# Time Blocking and Planning


> ⚠️ Note: Canva design title is "Lesson 28" but content is titled "Lesson Plan Muscle Power". Using folder name as canonical; muscle-power body content appears to be a residual header.

## Learning Objectives
To understand time as a sacred trust (Amanah) from Allah; to learn the practical skill of time blocking for a balanced life based on Islamic priorities; to cultivate the discipline and self-accountability required of principled leaders; and to prioritize tasks based on Islamic values over worldly urgency.
