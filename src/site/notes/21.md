# The Art of Disputation

> [!info] Learning Objectives
> Critical thinking (*Tadabbur*) · Mastering Islamic debate (*Munazara*) · Public speaking & persuasive argumentation · Strengthening convictions under challenge · Practicing etiquette of disagreement (*Adab al-Bahth*).

## Activity 1: The Scholar's Blade

> [!quote] What is Munazara? (Murabbi Briefing)
> Explain the Islamic science of debate. Its goal is **not** to "win" for ego, but to collaboratively seek truth (*Haqq*). Contrast this with modern Western debate which often prioritizes sophistry, manipulation, and winning at any cost. Our tradition is rooted in sincerity (*Ikhlas*).

> [!important] The Rules of Engagement (Adab al-Bahth)
> - Attack the argument, not the person.
> - Listen respectfully without interrupting.
> - Define terms clearly.
> - Acknowledge valid points made by your opponent.
> - Intention must be seeking truth, not humiliating your brother.

### Debate Setup & Flow

- **Groups & Stances:** Groups of 4 divided into 2-man squads: **Affirmation (*Ithbāt*)** vs **Negation (*Nafy*)**.
- **Phase 1 (30 Mins):** Squad argument preparation & counter-point planning.
- **Phase 2 (20 Mins):** Structured debate flow (Opening 2m each → Rebuttal 1 2m each → Rebuttal 2 2m each → Closing 2m each).
- **Phase 3:** Murabbi verdict and feedback on reasoning and Adab.

> [!success] Judging Rubric (20 Points)
> - **Strength of Argument / Hujjah (/5)**
> - **Rebuttal & Takedown (/5)**
> - **Adherence to Adab (/5)**
> - **Clarity & Delivery / Bayan (/5)**
