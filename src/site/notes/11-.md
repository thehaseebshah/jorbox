# Adrenaline Arena

> [!info] Learning Objectives
> Physical fitness, Motor skills development, Hand-eye coordination, Spatial awareness, Strategic thinking, Problem-solving, Teamwork, Communication, Discipline, Resilience, Self-confidence, Sportsmanship.

## General Instructions
Choose only one of the following activities. But if time permits and Shabab get bored, you can also conduct the other one. We include multiple activities for three reasons: A/B Testing, adding variation to counter potential boredom, and to fill out the gap for the skills lesson if an activity is completed very early.

## Activity 1: Rugby

### Game Setup

- **Teams:** 2 teams, each consisting of 6 or more players.
- **Field:** Mark halfway line, scoring lines, and scoring cones.
- **Ball:** Use any football (no need for a rugby ball).

### How to Play

- **Objective:** The goal is to get the ball past the opponent's scoring line and place it on the ground in the scoring zone to earn 5 points.
- **Starting Play:** A coin toss decides the starting team. The game begins from the halfway line.
- **Moving the Ball:** Players can run with the ball or pass it backward/sideways only (no forward passes).
- **Tackling:** A tackle occurs when a player grabs the player with the ball to stop movement. The tackled player must immediately drop the ball.
- **Time Limit Rule:** A player can hold the ball for a maximum of 5 seconds before passing.
- **Game Time:** Two halves of 10 minutes each.

### Safety First

- Teach safe, controlled tackling (start gently).
- No rough play. Emphasize teamwork, passing, and fun.

## Activity 2: Handball

### Game Setup

- **Teams:** 2 teams of 6 players.
- **Field:** Mark a 20x10m rectangle with goal areas and a 3-step square "no-entry zone" around each goal.

### How to Play

- **Objective:** Score by throwing the ball into opponent's goal (1 point).
- **Ball Movement:** Players can dribble freely. Take up to 3 steps while holding the ball.
- **Goal Area Rule:** Only the goalkeeper can enter the 3-step box. Shooters throw from outside.
- **Time Rule:** Max 3 seconds holding the ball without dribbling.
