# Frisbee

## How to Play
- **Age Limit:** 8+
- **Group Size:** 4+
- **Duration:** 30 minutes
- **Prep Time:** < 5 minutes
- **Difficulty:** Easy
- **Rating:** 4

### Variations
- **Variation 1:** [How to Play Ultimate Frisbee](https://www.wikihow.com/Play-Ultimate-Frisbee)
- **Variation 2:** Qaidi Qaidi (Popular game)
