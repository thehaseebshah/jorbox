# Night Manhunt w. Torches/Whistles

## How to Play
- **Age Limit:** 14+
- **Group Size:** 5–10 players
- **Duration:** 99 minutes
- **Prep Time:** < 5 minutes
- **Difficulty:** 4
- **Code:** L-250

### Items Needed
- Role Cards (e.g., Mujahid/Jasoos)
- Mission Cards (Success/Fail)
- Voting Tokens (Approve/Reject)
- Can be handmade
