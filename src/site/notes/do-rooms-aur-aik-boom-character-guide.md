# Do Rooms aur Aik Boom — Character Guide

- **Source:** Shabab Skills / Activities / Party
- **Design ID:** `DAG_hX_JMao`
- **Edit URL:** https://www.canva.com/d/kMLAn6IgoW4OX7b
- **View URL:** https://www.canva.com/d/z-UI7Qkwsw6DKmY

*Original game: Two Rooms and a Boom by Alan Gerding & Sean McCoy (Tuesday Knight Games, 2014).*

Character guide for the game. Each character has a win condition, special powers, and conditions. Characters are split between Blue Team and Red Team, with various neutrals, primary characters, and support roles.

## Blue Team

- **President** — Blue Team wins if you do not gain the "dead" condition.
- **Blue Team** — Win if the President does not gain the "dead" condition. Stars denote Blue Team.
- **President's Daughter** — Backup character for the President (if buried).
- **Vice President** — *Note: Sent by the 2025 VP character; not part of base game.*
- **Martyr** — You win if the Sniper shoots you at the end of the last round.
- **Doctor** — Reduces President win risk through card share (paired with Engineer).
- **Nurse** — Backup character for the Doctor (if buried).
- **Angel** — Immune variant — immune to all powers and conditions.
- **Invincible** — Immune condition (similar to Angel).
- **Butler** — You win if in same room as Maid and President at end.
- **Maid** — You win if in same room as Butler and President at end.
- **Romeo** — You win if in same room as Juliet and Bomber at end.
- **Juliet** — You win if in same room as Romeo and Bomber at end.
- **Wife** — You win if in same room as President and Mistress is not.
- **Mistress** — You win if in same room as President and Wife is not.
- **Ahab** — You win if Moby is in the same room as the Bomber at the end and you are not.
- **Moby** — Same as Ahab.
- **Decoy** — You win if the Sniper shoots you at the end of the last round.
- **Intern** — You win if in same room as President at end of game.
- **Survivor** — You win if NOT in same room as Bomber at end.
- **Victim** — You win if in same room as Bomber at end.

## Red Team

- **Bomber** — Primary character. Everyone in same room at end gets "dead" condition.
- **Red Team** — Win if the President gains the "dead" condition. Bombs denote Red Team.
- **Bomb-Bot** — Designed to be exploded (backup).
- **Dr. Boom** — Aggro Red Team win condition; instant end if card-share with President.
- **Tuesday Knight** — Backup character for the Bomber.
- **Criminal** — Forces "shy" condition on card share.
- **Coy Boy** — Forced to "coy" condition (only color share).
- **Shy Guy** — Forced to "shy" condition (no card share).
- **Conman** — Converts color share to private reveal.
- **Cupid** — Forces "in love" condition on 2 players.
- **Eris** — Forces "in hate" condition on 2 players.
- **Doppelganger** — *(Not in detail in the export.)*
- **Spy** — Color of opposite team. Works well with Coy Boy.
- **Anarchist** — Win if your vote helped usurp a leader in majority of rounds.
- **Paranoid** — Forced to "paranoid" condition (card share only, once per game).
- **Mastermind** — Win if you are a room leader at end AND were opposing leader at some point.
- **Minion** — Win if a leader is never usurped in the same room as you.
- **Bouncer** — Once/round, force one player to switch rooms.
- **Engineer** — Card share with Bomber required for Red Team to win.
- **Tinkerer** — Backup for Engineer.
- **Tinkerer Error** — *(Not in detail.)*

## Neutral / Special

- **Agoraphobe** — Difficult to win. Win if you never leave your initial room.
- **Agoraphobe's Twin** — *(Not in detail.)*
- **Traveler** — Win if sent to a different room as hostage at end of MOST rounds.
- **Demon** — "Liar" condition (always lie).
- **Hot Potato** — Trades cards with anyone who shares with you.
- **Leprechaun** — Trades cards; wins at end of game.
- **Robot** — Wins if first share fails to win.
- **Clone** — Wins if first share wins.
- **Mummy** — Forces "cursed" condition (no noise).
- **Mime** — No noise.
- **Blind** — Don't open eyes.
- **Drunk** — Trades card with "sober" card at last round.
- **Cupid** — Forces "in love" condition.
- **Eris** — Forces "in hate" condition.
- **Gambler** — Correctly guess winning team at end.
- **Private Eye** — Correctly guess buried character card at end.
- **Sniper** — Shoot the Target at end of last round.
- **Target** — Win if Sniper doesn't shoot you.
- **Papparazzo** — Annoying; prevent private conversations.
- **Paparazzo** — *(Alt spelling.)*
- **Negotiator** — "Savvy" condition (card share only).
- **Nuclear Tyrant** — Solo win: if President and Bomber don't card share with you.
- **Usurper** — Public reveal + become leader mid-round.
- **Zombie** — Contagious; everyone becomes a zombie.
- **Thug** — Forces "coy" condition on card share.
- **Hug Bomber** — If shares with Bomber, everyone except President in room gets "dead".

## Game Mechanics

- **Card Share / Color Share:** Revealing your card or character color to another player.
- **Private Reveal Power:** Verbally reveal your card privately to one player.
- **Public Reveal Power:** Show your card to everyone.
- **Acting:** Must perform a behavior (no noise, eyes closed, etc.).
- **Pause Game:** Pause the game at end of last round to make an announcement.
- **Bury:** Character card is not dealt; a backup character takes its role.
- **Win conditions override:** Conditions like "in love" replace a player's primary win objective.
- **Contradictory conditions cancel:** e.g. "shy" + "foolish" = neither.
