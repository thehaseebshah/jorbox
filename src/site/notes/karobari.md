# Karobari

## How to Play
**Inspired by:** Snake Oil / Snake Oil mode, People are given a problem and they draw a solution mode

- **Age Limit:** 10+
- **Group Size:** 3+
- **Duration:** 30 minutes
- **Prep Time:** < 15 minutes
- **Difficulty:** 5
- **Items Needed:** Cards
- **Rating:** 5
- **Code:** P-010

### Mode

- Snake Oil mode (people are given a problem and they draw a solution)
