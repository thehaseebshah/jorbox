# Hands of Ihsan

## Learning Objectives
To develop imagination and creativity through Islamic themes; to practice structuring a clear beginning, middle, and end in storytelling; to improve communication and confidence in narrating; to learn the importance of clarity, relevance, and moral purpose in stories; and to strengthen brotherhood by listening respectfully and encouraging one another's creativity.

## Activity 1: The Artisan Challenge

### Setup

Shabab do not know beforehand that they will be crafting.

They must use:

- Random items they brought from home (bags, bottles, wrappers, stationery, etc.).
- Natural materials they find in the park (sticks, leaves, stones, sand, grass).

### How to Play

- Divide into individuals or small teams (2–3 members).
- Give 20–25 minutes to design and build something using only the materials at hand.
- At the end, each team presents their creation and explains its use, symbolism, or story.
- Murabbi highlights the creativity and lessons of resourcefulness.

### Scoring (out of 25)

- Creativity & originality – 5 points
- Clear function or story behind the craft – 5 points
- Neatness & effort – 5 points
- Use of limited resources – 5 points
- Presentation & confidence – 5 points

### Idea Prompts for Shabab (Examples Murabbi can share if they're stuck)

**Practical / Useful**

- A pen holder or organizer.
- A small bag or pouch.
- A phone stand or book holder.
- A simple water filter (stones, sand, cloth – symbolic, not for real use).
- A mini shelf or stand from sticks.

**Symbolic / Models**

- A masjid or minaret model.
- A bridge (representing brotherhood or connection).
- A simple tent or shelter (reminding of hijrah or travelers).
- A boat or ark (like Nuh عليه السلام).
- A compass or map (symbolizing guidance).

**Artistic / Decorative**

- A leaf collage.
- A stone or sand mandala pattern.
- A wind chime from bottles/cans/sticks.
- A miniature "garden" using soil and plants.
- A wall-hanging made of string and natural objects.

**Fun / Imaginative**

- A toy (ball, slingshot, marble maze).
- A catapult (safe/small scale).
- A "future invention" prototype.
- A robot/vehicle made from bottles or boxes.
- An animal model (camel, bird, fish).

### Murabbi Notes

- Emphasize creativity over perfection.
- Remind Shabab: even Sahabah and great leaders used what they had with Amanah and skill.
- Encourage short reflections: "What does this object represent?" or "How could this help someone?"
