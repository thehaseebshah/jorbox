# Cooking Challenge

> [!info] Learning Objectives
> Practical life skills · Teamwork and cooperation · Following instructions · Creativity & presentation · Time management · Ethical reasoning · Adab and tarbiyah · Decolonized worldview · Problem-solving · Respect for *ni'mah* and *rizq* · Revival of Sunnah-based cooking practices.

## Activity 1: Cooking Challenge

Each cooking group cooks a meal/dish using available resources. Each group appoints an Ameer and **must follow the Mashwarat process**.

> [!important] Challenge Logistics
> - **Planning:** Proper planning & Mashawrat mandatory before lighting fires or preparing ingredients.
> - **Time Limit:** 40 Minutes cooking phase.
> - **Brotherhood Dastarkhwan:** 15 Minutes. Every group shares their food on a joint *Dastarkhwan* so all Shabab eat together.
> - **Murabbi Evaluation:** Note individual performance for student profiles.

> [!success] Judging Criteria
> Murabbis / Park Admin judge winners based on:
> 1. Taste & Efficiency
> 2. Uniqueness & Limited Resource Utilization
> 3. Creativity & Presentation
> 4. Quality of Mashwarat & Ameer Obedience
> 5. Team Coordination & Brotherhood

> [!tip] Sample Food Ideas
> *Rice, Sabzi, Samosa, Kebabs, Meetha, Salan, BBQ, Burgers, Sandwiches, Fresh Salads, Rotis, Rolls, or any innovative recipe!*
