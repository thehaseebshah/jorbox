# Cooking Challenge


## Learning Objectives

Practical life skills, Teamwork and cooperation, Following instructions and sequencing, Creativity and presentation, Time management, Ethical reasoning, Adab and tarbiyah, Decolonized worldview, Interdisciplinary thinking, Problem-solving, Respect for ni'mah and rizq, Revival of Sunnah-based cooking practices.

## Activity 1 — Cooking

Each cooking group will have to cook a meal, food or dish with the help of available resources. Each group will be having an Ameer. Mashwarat process is must to follow.

Please make sure they have taken food material along equipment for cooking.

Instruct Shababs that they have to do proper planning & Mushawarat to prepare the best possible food.

Please note down performance of each shabab during the activity & update student profile sheets.

- **Time Limit:** 40 Mins
- After completion, each group will share food with other groups & everyone will eat together on dastarkhwan.
- **Eating & Judgment of winner:** 15 Mins

Murabbis or Park Admin will taste food to announce the winner based on taste, efficiency, Uniqueness, availability of limited resources, creativity, Mashawrat, Ameer following, planning, strategy & Team work.

Everyone will eat prepared food together. Everyone will share the food with each other.

### Sample food ideas

Rice, Sabzi, Samosa, Kebabs, Meetha, Salan, BBQ, Burger, Sandwich, Salad, Roti, Roll, Any Unique or new food.
