## How to Play
**Inspired by:** Guess the Scene + Guess the Drawing / + Guess the Scene + Guess the Drawing

- **Duration:** 30 minutes
- **Prep Time:** < 5 minutes
- **Difficulty:** 4
- **Code:** P-390

### Items Needed

- Role Cards (e.g., Mujahid/Jasoos)
- Mission Cards (Success/Fail)
- Voting Tokens (Approve/Reject)
