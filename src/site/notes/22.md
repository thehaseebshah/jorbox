# Declamation

> [!info] Learning Objectives
> Eloquent expression · Adab in speech · Prophetic communication principles · Spiritual reflection · Moral reasoning · Emotional regulation · Leadership presence · Persuasive rhetoric · Vocal control · Active listening · Audience engagement · Public speaking composure.

## Activity 1: Declamation Contest

> [!note] Stage & Audience Setup
> - **The Stage:** Defined podium or designated speaking spot facing the room.
> - **The Audience:** Remaining Shabab sit formally. Part of Adab training is listening attentively without distraction.
> - **The Jury:** 2–3 Murabbis form the evaluation panel.

### Event Timeline

1. **Pre-Announcement (1 Week Prior):** Participants draw or choose a topic to write, memorize, and practice at home.
2. **Order of Declamation (Min 0–5):** Random draw for speaking order. Walk up with composure and offer Salam.
3. **Delivery (3–4 Mins):** Speak with tone, pacing, body language, and controlled emotion.
4. **Timekeeper:** Front-row visual signals for time awareness.

> [!quote] Sample Declamation Themes
> - **The Letter to the Soul:** A passionate call to the self (*Nafs*) to awaken from heedlessness (*Ghaflah*).
> - **Reviving the Legacy:** Overcoming apathy and reclaiming the honor of the Ummah.
> - **Standing for Justice:** Defense of the oppressed, inspired by companions like Bilal ibn Rabah or Husayn ibn Ali (RA).
> - **The Echo of the Minbar:** Re-delivering historic addresses (e.g. Saladin's speech before liberating Jerusalem).

> [!success] Judging Rubric (Out of 50 Points)
> - 🏛️ **Presence & Adab (10 pts):** Salam, posture, eye contact, respectful demeanor.
> - 🎙️ **Vocal Modulation (10 pts):** Control of volume, pitch, and deliberate pauses.
> - 🗣️ **Clarity & Articulation (10 pts):** Clear pronunciation and rhythmic flow.
> - ❤️ **Emotional Depth (10 pts):** Genuine passion and solemnity matching the text.
> - 💡 **Structure & Impact (10 pts):** Powerful opening hook and memorable conclusion.
