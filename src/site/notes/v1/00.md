> [!info] Learning Objectives
> Social bonding, Icebreaking and confidence-building, Communication skills, Team spirit, Listening and attention, Understanding group values, Familiarity with Murabbis and peers, Active engagement, Internalization of mission and vision, Emotional connection to Shabab identity, Respect for structure and mentorship.
