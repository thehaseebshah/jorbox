# Navigators

> [!info] Learning Objectives
> Developing clear, concise, and precise verbal communication under pressure; cultivating active listening and the ability to follow instructions (Ittiba) faithfully; building trust (Amanah) and reliance between team members; and enhancing team-based problem-solving when faced with obstacles.
