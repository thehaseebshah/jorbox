# Team Building Activities

> [!info] Learning Objectives
> Teamwork and coordination, Problem-solving, Communication skills, Patience and cooperation, Leadership and support roles, Trust-building, Spatial awareness, Emotional intelligence, Adab and tarbiyah, Physical and mental resilience, Strategic thinking, Group harmony, Situational awareness.

### Preface
What Shabab will get after completion of 6 months, what will be the benefits? *(For Murabbi Reading)*

Tell them that all the learnings in the skills class will be achieved with the help of activities, games & challenges.

Mention below are few important attributes & improvements that Shabab will be able to achieve with 100% attendance only:

- Confidence & strong Moral character
- Clear ideology & direction
- Strong Muslim Identity
- Better Momin as per Quran & Sunnah
- To understand responsibilities as a Muslim
- To understand your real value & status
- Self management & discipline
- Clear career & life goals that are well aligned with Purpose of life
- Empower you to make the best decision for your future
- Skills needed to Perform better in universities with strong personality
- Essential Skills for daily activities

### Human Knot
#### How to Play
Watch [this video guide](https://www.canva.com/design/DAGrcbXXAQg/kXYsSv2Zvi15-W-3-MMQkQ/watch).

Ask questions to Shabab such as:

- What strategies did you use to untangle the knot?
- How did you communicate without speaking?
- What did you learn about teamwork and cooperation?

What are other learnings did they get from this activity:

- You will see that there will be a leader in your Shabab group who will be leading.
- That activity will be icebreaking for Shabab with the fun in activity enabling them to start developing relations with each other.
- Shabab will be able to learn how they can solve problems together in a team with cooperation.
