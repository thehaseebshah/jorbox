# Map Activity

## Learning Objectives
To cultivate spatial awareness and creativity by constructing a physical map from available materials; to strengthen teamwork through collaborative design and decision-making; to connect geography with Islamic history and the struggles of the Ummah; to improve memory and recall of significant places mentioned in the Qur'an, Seerah, and contemporary events; to sharpen critical thinking by linking locations with Nazriati (ideological) questions; and to foster pride, responsibility, and brotherhood in understanding the geography of the Muslim world.

## Trivia Questions List
- **Where was Prophet Yusuf عليه السلام thrown into a well?** → Near Dothan, close to modern-day Nablus, Palestine.
- **Where did Prophet Yusuf عليه السلام migrate after being sold?** → Egypt (Misr, modern-day Egypt).
- **Where did Musa عليه السلام receive revelation on the mountain?** → Mount Sinai, Egypt/Sinai Peninsula.
- **Where did the Ark of Nuh عليه السلام come to rest?** → Mount Judi, in modern-day Turkey (Şırnak Province).
- **Where did Ibrahim عليه السلام build the Kaaba with Ismail عليه السلام?** → Makkah, Saudi Arabia.
- **Where was Prophet Yunus عليه السلام swallowed by the whale?** → Near Nineveh, modern-day Mosul, Iraq.
- **Where was the people of 'Ad destroyed?** → Al-Ahqaf, Empty Quarter desert (modern Yemen/Oman border).
- **Where was Prophet Salih's قوم ثمود (Thamud) located?** → Al-Hijr, modern-day Al-Ula, Saudi Arabia.
- **Where was the people of Lut destroyed?** → Around the Dead Sea, modern-day Jordan/Palestine border.
- **Where did Maryam عليها السلام retreat to give birth to Isa عليه السلام?** → Bethlehem, modern-day Palestine.
- **Where was the capital of the Umayyad Caliphate?** → Damascus, modern-day Syria.
- **Where was the capital of the Abbasid Caliphate?** → Baghdad, modern-day Iraq.
- **Where was Al-Andalus ruled from?** → Cordoba and later Granada, modern-day Spain.
- **Where was Salahuddin Ayyubi's great victory against the Crusaders?** → Battle of Hattin, near Tiberias, Palestine.
- **Where did Tariq ibn Ziyad land when he entered Spain?** → Gibraltar, southern Spain.
- **Where was the Ottoman Empire centered?** → Istanbul (formerly Constantinople), modern-day Turkey.
- **Where did the Mongols sack Baghdad in 1258?** → Baghdad, Iraq.
- **Where did the Mughal Empire rise?** → Delhi, modern-day India.
- **Where was the capital of the Fatimid Caliphate?** → Cairo, Egypt.
- **Where was the great library of Al-Qarawiyyin founded?** → Fez, Morocco.
- **Where is Gaza located?** → Gaza Strip, Palestine.
- **Where did the Bosnian war and Srebrenica massacre take place?** → Srebrenica, modern-day Bosnia and Herzegovina.
- **Where is Kashmir located?** → Northern region divided between India, Pakistan, and China.
- **Where are the Rohingya Muslims being persecuted?** → Rakhine State, modern-day Myanmar (Burma).
- **Where are the Uyghur Muslims facing oppression?** → Xinjiang province, modern-day China.
- **Where is Chechnya, a region that fought for independence?** → North Caucasus, modern-day Russia.
- **Where was the U.S. invasion that toppled Saddam Hussein?** → Iraq (Baghdad as the capital).
- **Where was the Syrian Civil War and ongoing conflict centered?** → Syria (Aleppo, Damascus, Homs).
- **Where did the Arab Spring start in North Africa?** → Tunisia (Sidi Bouzid).
- **Where are Muslims in Central Africa facing conflict?** → Central African Republic.
- **Where did the massacre at Sabra and Shatila refugee camps take place?** → Beirut, modern-day Lebanon.
- **Where are Palestinians forced into refugee camps in neighboring countries?** → Jordan, Lebanon, and Syria.
- **Where is the Al-Aqsa Mosque located, the heart of many modern conflicts?** → Jerusalem, Palestine.
- **Where is the modern conflict in Darfur?** → Western Sudan.
- **Where are the Tatar Muslims indigenous to, who were deported by Stalin?** → Crimea, modern-day Ukraine.
- **Where are the Somali Muslims suffering from famine and warlordism?** → Somalia, Horn of Africa.
- **Where is Afghanistan?** → Afghanistan (Kabul as capital).
