# Survival RPG

> [!info] Learning Objectives
> Observation skills and attention to detail, Strategic planning and time management, Team coordination and communication, Problem-solving under time pressure, Physical activity and spatial awareness, Resourcefulness and creative thinking, Following instructions with precision (Ittiba), Building trust and reliability within teams (Amanah), and Strengthening brotherhood (Ukhuwwah) through collaborative achievement, survival skills.

We will be doing scavenger hunt again but this time with a survival theme. Read the scavenger hunt activity guidelines again and also watch [this](Watch: https://www.loom.com/share/c0830bb592b048dd945262b11a22f960) guide on survival theming.
