##### Important Note
Do not use the handicap mechanism of making a player unable to use both legs and hands. So overall in the group of 4 there will be 4 handicap mechanisms:
- Blindfold
- Inability to use one leg
- Inability to use both arms
- Inability to speak

Whenever a shabab breaks the handicap rule, intentionally or by mistake, freeze the group for 10 seconds.
