# Vocab Day

## Learning Objectives
Expanding the arsenal of words (Lughah) for effective Da'wah and clear expression (Bayan), enhancing mental agility and quick thinking (Firasah) under pressure, fostering creative problem-solving within set constraints, and strengthening brotherhood (Ukhuwwah) through spirited competition.

## Activity 1: Categories

### Category List

Prophets Mentioned in the Qur'an, Drinks, Things a Man Should Know How to Fix, Companions of the Prophet ﷺ (Sahaba), Famous Islamic Battles, Fruits & Vegetables, Good Character Traits (Emaani Sifaat), Things That Are Haram, Animals Found in Pakistan, Surahs of the Qur'an (Without the "Al", so surah names will be like Fatiha Maryam etc.), Things Found in a Masjid, Muslim Scholars or Scientists, Excuses for Being Late to Shabab (Only one word excuses), Cities of Pakistan, Famous Pakistani Dishes, Countries with a Muslim Majority, Good Muslim Names, Fields Muslims Should Excel In, Things in a Kitchen, Skills Your Grandfather Had That You Don't, Acts of Kindness (Ihsan), Items of Men's Clothing, Tools a Man Should Own, Challenges Facing the Ummah Today, Reasons to Be Grateful to Allah (One word answers only), Overly Complicated Names for Simple Things, Things You Shout During Football, Things That Disappear When Needed, Things Found Under a Car Seat, Things You Can Trip Over (Jin ko lag ke banda gir jaye), Nicknames for Friends, Bad Gift Ideas, Spices That Make You Sneeze, Things a Cat Thinks It Owns, Names of Colors, Sports, Parts of the Body, Occupations or Professions, Types of Birds, Items of Furniture, Hobbies, Weather Conditions, Things Found in a School, Types of Fish, Types of Flowers, Metals, Things That Can Be Sharp, Things That Can Be Broken, Things That Grow, Things That Have Layers, Things That Have Teeth, Things That Have a Voice, Things That Have a Heart.
