### Public Speaking Tips
#### How to Play
### Instructions

Watch [this video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Iqq1roF4C8s) first and let Shabab watch it in the park.

Then read and understand the following strategies Muhammad Qahtani used in this winning talk, and teach them to Shabab while breaking down the video:

Mohammed Qahtani’s award-winning Toastmasters speech, "The Power of Words," is widely considered a masterclass in public speaking. By analyzing his delivery and structure, we can map out the exact public speaking techniques and tricks he used to win the World Championship:

##### 1. The Subversive "Hook" (Visual Incongruity)
Qahtani opens his speech by stepping up to the microphone, pulling out a cigarette, and putting a lighter to it.
- **The Trick:** This completely shatters expectations. Doing something seemingly prohibited or shocking forces the audience to immediately snap to attention.
- **The Follow-Up:** After letting the tension sit for a second, he drops the punchline: "What? Or do you all think smoking kills?". He hooks the room instantly with curiosity.

##### 2. Strategic Use of Humor to Build Trust
Throughout the first half of the speech, he feeds the audience "alternative facts" (e.g., that tobacco farming is the real reason for the war on smoking, or that sugar is three times more deadly).
- **The Trick:** He uses a deadpan, authoritative delivery to make a ridiculous claim, only to pivot and reveal that he made it all up just to prove how easily words can manipulate people.

##### 3. Immersive "Play-Acting" and Stage Movement
Qahtani doesn’t just tell stories; he acts them out, shifting his physical location on stage to mark a transition into a new narrative space.
- **The Trick:** When talking about his 4-year-old son drawing on the wall, he physically kneels on the stage floor to simulate talking down to a child.
- **Vocal Sound Effects:** During a critical, emotional story about a friend in the hospital, he mimics the audio environment ("beep, beep, beeeeeep" and yelling "Clear!").

##### 4. Flawless Emotional Pivots (Humor to Pathos)
Great speeches do not stay on one emotional frequency. Qahtani expertly takes the audience on an emotional rollercoaster.
- **The Trick:** He transitions seamlessly from lighthearted, laughing scenarios to a devastating, highly personal climax about a friend dying because of cold, unsupportive words from a father.

##### 5. Rhetorical Repetition (Anaphora)
As he approaches the emotional peak of his message, he uses rhythmic, structural repetition:
- **The Trick:** He states: "Words have power. Words are power. Words could be your power."

##### 6. The "Call to Action" and Full-Circle Close
A classic speech rule is to tie the ending back to the beginning to provide the audience with a sense of closure.
- **The Trick:** He brings back the cigarette prop at the very end to anchor his final message: "Your mouth can spit venom, or it can mend a broken soul. Ladies and gentlemen, let that be our goal."

### Bol keh Lab Azaad hen Tere
#### How to Play
**Inspired by:** Impromptu Speaking

##### Setup
- **Groups Formation:** Divide Shabab into groups of 10 (8-12 acceptable).
- **Speaking Order:** Sit in a circle, number participants 1-10 to establish order.
- **Topic Pool:** Prepare 15-20 topic cards with funny, desi-themed topics.
- **Timer Setup:** Have a visible timer or stopwatch ready.
- **Audience Arrangement:** Other groups sit as audience to listen and provide supportive feedback.

##### Round Structure
- Each speaker gets exactly 2 minutes to speak.
- Each speaker gets exactly 2 minutes to prepare.
- Preparation and speaking happen simultaneously (parallel processing).

##### The Process
- **Step 1 (Topic Assignment):** Speaker #1 draws a random topic card and begins their 2-minute preparation immediately.
- **Step 2 (Parallel Preparation):** As Speaker #1 starts speaking, Speaker #2 draws their topic card and begins preparing.
- **Step 3 (Seamless Transition):** When Speaker #1's 2 minutes are up, Speaker #2 begins speaking while Speaker #3 draws their topic.
- **Step 4 (Continuous Flow):** Pattern continues until all 10 speakers complete their turns.

##### Speaking Rules
- Must speak for the full 2 minutes (no stopping early).
- Must stay on topic throughout.
- Can use humor, personal anecdotes, or creative storytelling.
- Must be respectful and appropriate.
- Urdu/English mix is encouraged for authentic desi flavor.

##### Audience Engagement
- Audience must remain silent during speeches.
- Supportive reactions (clapping, nodding) are encouraged.
- No heckling or interruptions allowed.

##### Winning & Scoring
- **Fluency:** Smooth speaking without long pauses.
- **Creativity:** Original and engaging content.
- **Humor:** Successfully incorporated funny elements.
- **Cultural Touch:** Effective use of desi references.
- **Time Management:** Effective use of full 2 minutes.
- **Scoring:** 1-5 per category (max 25 points). Winner is highest-scoring speaker.
