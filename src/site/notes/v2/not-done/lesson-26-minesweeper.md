# Lesson 26 — Minesweeper

- **Folder:** New Lessons / Not Done

## Learning Objectives

To physically experience the meaning of Taqwa by moving with caution and awareness of consequences; developing discernment (Firasah) and logical deduction to navigate a hidden path; understanding the immediate impact of missteps; and cultivating patience (Sabr) through a slow, deliberate process.

## Activity 1 — Minesweeper

### Important Note

Do not use the handicap mechanism of making a player unable to use both legs and hands. So overall in the group of 4 there will be 4 handicap mechanisms:

- Blindfold
- Inability to use one leg
- Inability to use both arms
- Inability to speak

Whenever a shabab breaks the handicap rule, intentionally or by mistake, freeze the group for 10 seconds.
