## Activity 1 — Ummah Innovation

### Setup

- **Group Formation:** Divide all Shabab into groups of 6-8 players. Each group plays independently.
- **Word Cards:** Prepare 30-40 word cards with nouns, adjectives, and concepts. Same set of cards can be used for multiple groups.
- **Circle Formation:** Each group sits in a circle on the ground/grass for easy interaction.
- **Player Hands:** Each player draws 6 word cards to form their starting hand.
- **Starting Judge:** In each group, choose a starting player to be the first "Judge" using any random method.
- **Scoring Setup:** One Murabbi per group keeps track of points on notepad.

### How to Play

**Theme:** Players are inventors in a thriving Islamic civilization, pitching revolutionary inventions to the Ministry of Innovation. Remember that Muslims were pioneers in science, mathematics, medicine, and technology - now it's your turn to continue that legacy!

### Round Structure

1. **Invention Creation:** All players except the Judge select exactly 2 cards from their hand to combine into a single invention. Read forward to understand how.
2. **Pitch Preparation:** Inventors have 1 minute to mentally prepare their 60-second pitch explaining their invention.
3. **The Pitching Session:** Each inventor gets exactly 60 seconds to pitch their invention to the Ministry of Innovation (represented by the Judge). They must explain:
   - What their invention does
   - How it would benefit muslims
   - Why it's revolutionary and innovative
4. **Ministry Decision:** After all pitches, the Judge (acting as the Ministry of Innovation) chooses the best invention. That inventor gets 1 point.
5. **Refill & Rotate:** All used word cards are discarded. Players draw new cards to return to 6 total. The judge role passes to the next player clockwise.
6. **Continue Until Complete:** Play continues until every player has been the Judge once.

### Sample Word Cards (30-40 total)

**Nouns:** Mirror, Carpet, Book, Compass, Lamp, Water, Horse, Star, Medicine, Gold, Paper, Wheel, Bridge, Tower, Ship, Garden, Clock, Key, Sword, Shield.

**Adjectives:** Flying, Magical, Golden, Healing, Invisible, Fast, Strong, Wise, Bright, Sacred, Ancient, Modern, Flexible, Powerful, Silent.

**Concepts:** Light, Sound, Time, Speed, Protection, Knowledge, Communication, Transportation, Health, Security, Beauty, Strength, Wisdom, Peace, Unity.

### Winning & Scoring

- **Victory Conditions:** The player with the most points (won rounds) at the end wins their group.
- **Tiebreaker:** If tied, the player who gave the most creative pitch (group vote) wins.

### Pitching Guidelines

Successful Pitch Elements:

- Clear explanation of how the two words combine
- Specific benefits for Islamic civilization
- Creative or humorous problem-solving approach
- Connection to Islamic values or history when possible
- Confident and enthusiastic delivery

### Sample Word Combinations

- "Flying" + "Carpet" = Aerial Transportation for Trade Routes
- "Healing" + "Mirror" = Diagnostic Tool for Physicians
- "Golden" + "Compass" = Precise Navigation Instrument
- "Invisible" + "Shield" = Advanced Protection System

### Strategic Tips

- **Be Creative:** Think outside the box when combining your two word cards
- **Be Confident:** Sell your idea with enthusiasm and conviction
- **Stay Focused:** Don't exceed the 60-second time limit
- **Think Practically:** Consider how your invention would actually work
