# Lesson 20 — Writing Activities Set 1

- **Folder:** New Lessons / Not Done

## Learning Objectives

Critical thinking from an Islamic paradigm, Articulating a robust Islamic worldview, Persuasive writing & public speaking, Identifying and deconstructing Western narratives, Strengthening Islamic identity, and Fostering intellectual brotherhood (Ukhuwwah) through shared ideas.

## Activity 1 — Counter-Narrative

### Setup

- *Prepare the Gauntlet:* The Murabbi prepares 5-10 topic cards with common anti-Islamic or secular arguments (e.g., "Tradition holds back progress," "Follow your desires to be free," "Religion is a private matter").
- *Arrange the Majlis:* Shabab sit in a semi-circle, ready to write.

This is an Essay Writing Activity.

### How to Participate

1. **The Spark (5 mins):** The Murabbi gives a brief, fiery introduction about the reality of intellectual warfare and the duty of a Muslim man to defend the truth.
2. **Draw Your Topic (1 min):** Each participant draws a topic card.
3. **Forge the Argument (15 mins):** Each participant has 15 minutes to write a sharp, concise refutation (100-150 words).
4. **Unleash the Kalam (20-30 mins):** One by one, each participant stands and reads their counter-narrative aloud. The Murabbi will provide immediate feedback and a score.

**Language of Submission:** Urdu

### Evaluation & Scoring

The Murabbi will score each written and delivered piece out of 20 points based on the following criteria:

| Criterion | Description | Points |
|---|---|---|
| Aqeedah & Conviction | How well does the argument reflect a strong, correct Islamic worldview? | / 5 |
| Clarity & Impact (Bayan) | How clearly and persuasively is the idea communicated? | / 5 |
| Use of Evidence (Daleel) | How effectively are Islamic principles or reasoning used to support the point? | / 5 |
| Adherence to Prompt | How well does the response directly address the topic on the card? | / 5 |
| **Total Score** | | **/ 20** |

## Activity 2 — A Legacy of the Lions

### Setup

- *Present the Prompts:* The Murabbi presents the two prompt options clearly on a whiteboard or printed sheets.
- *Set the Atmosphere:* The Murabbi should ensure a quiet, serious, and reflective environment. This is an exercise in taking account of oneself before one is taken into account.

### How to Participate

1. **The Reminder (5 mins):** The Murabbi gives a short, impactful talk on the meaning of legacy in Islam versus the West's focus on materialism and consumerism.
2. **Choose Your Path (1 min):** Each participant chooses one of the two prompts to respond to.
3. **The Ink Flows (25 mins):** Participants write their response, focusing on sincerity (Ikhlas) and deep reflection.
4. **The Circle of Brothers (10-15 mins):** The Murabbi asks for everyone to share what they wrote. The written sheets are then collected for scoring.

**Language of Submission:** Urdu

### Prompt Options (Choose One)

**Prompt A: The Final Verdict**

Task: Write the eulogy you hope a close, God-fearing friend would be able to give after your Janazah. What character, deeds, and impact would he speak of? Frame it as a one-page biography, focusing on the eulogy as the main text.

**Prompt B: Letter to My Son**

Task: Imagine your son is now 15 years old. Write a one-page letter to him, outlining the most important principles he must live by as a Muslim man. What is your deepest advice for navigating this world with his Deen intact?

### Evaluation & Scoring

The Murabbi will score the chosen written piece out of 20 points based on the following criteria:

| Criterion | Description | Points |
|---|---|---|
| Islamic Aspiration | How well does the piece reflect deep Islamic values over worldly success? | / 5 |
| Sincerity & Depth | How much sincere self-reflection and depth of thought is apparent? | / 5 |
| Clarity of Legacy | How clearly is the desired character, advice, or impact articulated? | / 5 |
| Adherence to Prompt | How well does the writing fulfill the requirements of the chosen prompt? | / 5 |
| **Total Score** | | **/ 20** |
