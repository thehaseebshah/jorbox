## Reading Material: Tips for Murabbi to develop Communication Skills in Shabab
Developing communication skills in Shabab is an important aspect of their overall development. Here are some tips for Murabbi to help Shabab improve their communication skills:

1. **Encourage participation:** Encourage Shabab to participate in class discussions, debates, and other activities that require them to speak in front of others. This will help them gain confidence in expressing their thoughts and ideas.
2. **Provide feedback:** Provide constructive feedback to Shabab on their communication skills. This will help them understand what they are doing well and what they need to improve on.
3. **Teach active listening:** Encourage Shabab to actively listen to their peers and ask questions to clarify their understanding. This will help them become better communicators by improving their ability to understand and respond to others.
4. **Encourage reading and writing:** Encourage Shabab to read and write regularly. This can help them develop their vocabulary and improve their ability to express themselves in written and oral communication.
5. **Model good communication:** Model good communication skills yourself as a teacher or educator. This can help Shabab learn by example and understand the importance of effective communication in their personal and professional lives.
