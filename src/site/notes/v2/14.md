### Spy Club

#### Setup

- **Players:** 4–15 Shabab (If there are more shabab than 15 then make more groups, each group will need their own topic pages.)
- **Topic Pages:** There are 10 topic pages, each containing a 4x4 grid of 16 related words. See the end of the activity instructions.
- **Murabbi:** The Murabbi is responsible for running the game and ensuring fairness throughout.

#### How to Play

##### 1. Start of the Round

**Choose the Topic Page:** The Murabbi selects one of the 10 topic pages with a 4x4 grid of 16 words. This page is placed in the center of the circle where all players can see it.

**Select the Secret Word:** The Murabbi chooses a word from the grid to be the secret word for that round.

**Write the Secret Word:** The Murabbi writes the secret word on a small piece of paper.

##### 2. Choosing the Spy & Word Reveal

The Murabbi selects one player to be the Spy.

The Murabbi goes around the circle, showing each player the small piece of paper with the secret word.

When it’s the Spy’s turn, the Murabbi secretly shows them the blank side of the paper. The Spy is the only player who does not know the secret word.

##### 3. Questioning Round (Round 1)

Players take turns asking one indirect question about the secret word. One player will ask another player and the other players after answering will ask another player.

**Example:** "Is it something used daily?", "Is it related to religion?", "Is it a type of object?"

All players (including the Spy) must answer truthfully, but subtly. It doesn't have to be a yes or no question. If it's a yes or no question you are also allowed to say, "It depends because... reason X"

The Spy listens closely to the answers and tries to deduce the secret word.

No follow-up questions are allowed. You can not ask the same person who questioned you another question. You must ask someone else.

This will last about 5 minutes. Use a timer.

##### 4. Discussion Round

Players discuss and debate who they think the Spy is. They may ask each other questions, analyze answers, and look for inconsistencies. The spy is not allowed to lie.

The Spy must act naturally, without revealing that they don’t know the word.

This will last about 2 minutes. Use a timer.

##### 5. Voting Round

After the discussion, players vote to identify who they believe the Spy is.

If the majority votes correctly, the Spy has one final chance to guess the secret word.

To vote, everyone raises their arms and points to someone who they think the spy is.

If the Spy is not identified by the majority, the Spy wins automatically.

#### Winning Conditions

##### The Spy Wins if:

- They are not identified by the majority vote.
- OR, they are identified, but then correctly guess the secret word.

##### The Non-Spies Win if:

They correctly identify the Spy AND the Spy fails to guess the secret word.

#### Scoring

After each round, points are awarded:

- **Spy wins:** The Spy gets 1 point.
- **Non-spies win:** The Non-spies as a team earn 1 point.

After a set number of rounds (e.g., 10-15), the player or team with the most points is declared the winner.

#### Rules & Strategy

##### Forbidden in the Game

- ❌ Lying (Even the Spy must not lie).
- ❌ Directly mentioning the secret word.
- ❌ Giving obvious hints.
- ❌ Breaking seriousness or revealing hints through jokes.
- ❌ Discussing who saw the paper and who didn’t.

##### Tips for Strategic Play

###### For the Spy:

- Pay close attention to how others answer questions.
- Build your understanding of the word from the clues provided.
- Be careful not to reveal your lack of knowledge in your own answers.

###### For Non-Spies:

- Ask broad questions to expose inconsistencies in the Spy’s behavior.
- Listen closely to every word, as even a slight slip-up can reveal the Spy.
- Don’t reveal too much with your clues—keep it general and indirect.

### Infiltration

#### Game Objective

The goal of the Operatives is to successfully complete 3 out of 5 missions. The goal of the Spies is to cause 3 missions to fail. The Spies know who their fellow saboteurs are, but the Operatives are in the dark, forced to rely on deduction and trust.

#### Players & Roles

- **Total Players:** 7 Shabab
- **Operatives (4):** Strive to complete missions successfully.
- **Spies (3):** Work quietly to fail missions.

Only the Murabbi knows the identity of the Spies.

#### Setup (Murabbi’s Tasks)

##### Prepare Materials

- **Role Slips:** Prepare 7 slips of paper: 4 labeled “Operative,” 3 labeled “Spy.”
- **Mission Cards:** Prepare 3 sets of mission cards. Each set should contain one "SUCCESS" card and one "FAIL" card.

##### Assign Roles

Secretly hand one role slip to each player. Players must keep their role secret.

##### Spies Reveal

Ask everyone to close their eyes.

Say, “All Spies, open your eyes and look at each other.” (Pause for a moment). “Now close your eyes.”

Say, “Everyone, open your eyes.”

##### Explain the Rules

Describe the game flow and answer any procedural questions. The game flow is described below.

#### Game Flow

The game consists of 5 rounds, or "Missions." Each mission follows these steps:

##### 1. Appoint the Mission Ameer

For the first mission, the Murabbi randomly assigns one player to be the Mission Ameer (leader).

For all subsequent missions, the role of Ameer passes one person to the left (clockwise).

##### 2. Propose a Team

The Ameer chooses exactly 3 players for the mission.

The Ameer can choose any players, including themselves. After selecting, the Ameer clearly announces the proposed team to the group.

##### 3. Vote on the Team

Every player (all 7) now votes on whether to approve the Ameer's proposed team.

The vote is public and simultaneous. On the Murabbi's count, players vote by hand:

- **Raise Hand:** Approve the team.
- **Lower Hand:** Reject the team.

If a majority (4 or more players) approves, the team goes on the mission. Proceed to Mission Execution.

If the team is rejected, the role of Ameer passes to the next player in clockwise order, and the process repeats from Step 2 (Propose a Team).

**Important:** If 5 teams are rejected in a row for a single mission, the Spy team automatically win the entire game.

##### 4. Mission Execution

The players on the approved team are each given two cards: one "SUCCESS" and one "FAIL" card.

Each team member secretly chooses one card to play for the mission and gives it to the Murabbi face-down.

- **Operative Members:** Must play a SUCCESS card.
- **Spy Members:** May play either a SUCCESS or a FAIL card.

The Murabbi collects the cards, shuffles them to keep them anonymous, and then reveals them to everyone.

If all cards are SUCCESS, the mission passes.

If one or more cards are FAIL, the mission fails.

The Murabbi should mark the outcome (Pass or Fail) on a public piece of paper. The role of Ameer then passes to the next player, and the next mission begins.

#### Victory Conditions

The Operatives win if 3 missions pass.

The Spy team wins if 3 missions fail.

(The Spy team also win instantly if 5 consecutive teams are rejected by vote).

#### Conduct & Fair Play

- No one may lie about their role or their secret mission vote.
- Players may discuss, question, or stay silent—but must not make false claims about their identity or actions.
- No hidden signals (e.g., notes, winks, text messages) are allowed.
- The Murabbi ensures smooth play and resolves any disputes.

#### Strategy Tips

##### For Operative Members:

- Track who consistently approves or rejects teams, especially when a Spy is likely on the team.
- Pay close attention to which players serve on failed missions. A failed mission with 3 players has at least one Spy.

##### For Spy Members:

- Occasionally vote to approve "good" teams (teams without Spy members) to avoid suspicion.
- Choose when to play a FAIL card carefully. Failing a mission too early can raise alarms.
