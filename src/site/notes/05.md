# Tallest Building (July 2026)

> [!info] Learning Objectives
> Problem Solving · Teamwork & Collaboration · Strategic Planning · Ameer Ki Itaat (Obedience to the Leader) · Creativity & Innovation (Ibda' wa Ibtikar) · Resource Management (Idarat al-Mawarid) · Resilience & Adaptability (Murunah wa Takayyuf) · Fostering an Appreciation for Nature (Taqdir al-Tabi'ah).

## Activity 1: Tallest Building

> [!note] Activity Setup
> - **Teams:** Divide the Shabab into small teams of 3–5 players.
> - **Materials:** Each team brings materials such as marshmallows (regular/mini), toothpicks/small sticks, construction paper, tape, rope, etc.
> - **Environment:** Gather participants in a park area rich with natural objects (leaves, rocks, pinecones, branches).
> - **Rules Briefing:** The goal is to build the tallest possible freestanding structure. **No single material used can be longer than 12 inches (1 foot)**, but objects can be joined together.

### How to Play

#### Brainstorming & Mashawrat (5 Mins)

Teams brainstorm and sketch their tower designs using the formal **Mashawrat process**. They discuss which natural objects to collect and how to integrate them for stability and height.

#### Building Phase (25 Mins)

> [!tip] Murabbi Observation
> The Murabbi sets a 25-minute limit. Evaluate each Shabab's contribution, teamwork, and problem-solving skills to complete student profiles. Encourage creative uses of natural objects.

### Winning Conditions

> [!success] Award Categories
> - 🏆 **Primary Winner:** Tallest freestanding tower measured from base to highest point.
> - 🎨 **Most Creative Design:** Most innovative and artistic tower.
> - 🌿 **Best Use of Natural Materials:** Best integration of park objects.
> - 🤝 **Strongest Teamwork:** Best collaboration and communication.

### Discussion and Reflection

> [!faq] Debrief Questions
> - What natural objects did you incorporate into your tower's design?
> - How did you decide to use these specific objects?
> - What challenges did you face while building, and how did you overcome them?
> - How did your team collaborate during the activity?

> [!warning] Cleanup and Nature Respect
> Remind participants to clean up their area and return any unused natural objects to where they were found. Stress the importance of respecting the environment and leaving the park clean (*Ihsan* towards nature).
