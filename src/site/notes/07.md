# Conflict Zone (July 2026)

> [!info] Learning Objectives
> Strategic thinking · Situational Awareness (*Firasah*) · Physical coordination & agility · Team-based problem-solving · Clear communication under pressure · Good sportsmanship · Upholding absolute honesty in gameplay (*Amanah*) · Fostering brotherhood (*Ukhuwwah*) through teamwork.

> [!note] General Instructions for Murabbi
> Choose only one of the following activities. If time permits and Shabab want more, conduct the second one. Multiple activities are provided for A/B testing, countering boredom, and filling time gaps.

## Activity 1: Operation: Virtue Scrolls

> [!note] Setup & Logistics
> - **Teams:** Divide Shabab into teams of 4. Murabbis can join to balance numbers.
> - **Virtue Stacks:** Each team has a base with a "Virtue Stack" of 10 folded papers in plain sight.
> - **Central Cache:** 10 additional Virtue Scrolls sit in the center of the field.
> - **Spawn Points:** A designated bag near each base serves as the spawn point.
> - **Ammunition:** Each player starts with 10 taped paper balls (A5 used paper).

### How to Play

> [!important] The Honesty Rule (Amanah)
> This is the core rule. Whenever you are hit by a paper ball, you must immediately call **"HIT!"** and drop any scroll you carry. If unsure, call hit anyway.

- **Objective:** Possess the most Virtue Scrolls in your stack when time expires (e.g. 15 mins).
- **Ghost Looting:** When hit, you become a "ghost" for 10 seconds. You cannot shoot or touch scrolls, but you can collect dropped ammo around you.
- **Respawn:** Return to your spawn bag and count 1 to 50 out loud before re-entering.

> [!warning] Forbidden Actions
> - Physical contact (no pushing, grabbing, tackling)
> - Moving team base or spawn bag
> - Carrying more than one Virtue Scroll at a time

> [!quote] Emaani Sifaat (Virtue Scroll Qualities)
> *Sabr (Patience), Shukr (Gratitude), Tawakkul (Trust in Allah), Ikhlas (Sincerity), Taqwa (God-consciousness), Sidq (Truthfulness), Amanah (Trustworthiness), Adl (Justice), Ihsan (Excellence), Haya (Modesty), Shuja'at (Courage), Istiqamah (Steadfastness), Yaqeen (Certainty), Tawbah (Repentance), Afw (Forgiveness), Himmah (High Ambition), Jihad al-Nafs (Struggle against self), Qana'ah (Contentment), Rahmah (Mercy), Ithar (Altruism), Firasah (Insight), Birr (Righteousness), Wafa (Loyalty), Hilm (Forbearance), Tadabbur (Contemplation).*

## Activity 2: Breach & Clear

> [!note] Tactical Scenario
> Attackers have infinite respawns (30-sec count). Defenders have 4 hits total per player (50-sec recovery) before elimination.

> [!success] Winning Conditions
> Time trial mode. Attackers must capture the virtue bag and return it to spawn faster than the opposing team when roles are switched.

> [!tip] Strategic Play Tips
> - **For Attackers:** Group up at spawn to launch coordinated waves. Use ghosting phase to stock up on dropped ammo.
> - **For Defenders:** Play cautiously behind cover. Prioritize targeting the player carrying the objective bag.
