# Conflict Zone (July 2026)

## Learning Objectives

Strategic thinking, Situational Awareness (Firasah), Physical coordination & agility, Team-based problem-solving, Clear and quick communication under pressure, Good sportsmanship, Upholding absolute honesty in gameplay (Amanah), and Fostering brotherhood (Ukhuwwah) through teamwork and healthy competition.

## General Instructions

Choose only one of the following activities. But if time permits and Shabab get bored, you can also conduct the other one. We include multiple activities for three reasons: A/B Testing, adding variation to counter potential boredom, and to fill out the gap for the skills lesson if an activity is completed very early.

## Activity 1: Operation: Virtue Scrolls

### Setup

- **Teams:** Divide the Shabab into teams of 4. There can be as many teams as needed. Murabbis can join teams to make the numbers even.
- **Bases & Virtue Stacks:** Each team chooses a starting corner or area for their base. In the center of their base, they will have their "Virtue Stack," a pile of folded papers in plain sight. Each team starts with 10 Virtue Scrolls in their stack.
- **Central Cache:** An additional 10 Virtue Scrolls are placed in a pile in the exact center of the field.
- **Spawn Points:** Each team places a bag near their base to serve as their designated spawn point.
- **Ammunition:** Each player begins with 10 paper balls wrapped in tape for ammunition. To create these, crumble sheets of used A5 sized paper into balls and wrap transparent tape around them.
- **Referees:** One or more Murabbis will act as timekeepers and referees to ensure fair play.

### How to Play

**Start of the Round:** On the Murabbi's signal, the game begins. Players can move anywhere on the field.

**The Objective:** The goal is to have the most Virtue Scrolls in your team's stack when the time runs out. You can acquire more scrolls by taking them from the central cache or by retrieving them from the stacks of other teams.

**Getting Hit & Respawning:**

1. **Call It:** If you are hit by a paper ball, you must immediately raise a hand and loudly call "HIT!" If you are carrying a Virtue Scroll, you must drop it on the spot.
2. **Ghost Looter:** For the next 10 seconds, you are a "ghost." You cannot be shot again, shoot anyone, or pick up Virtue Scrolls. However, you can move around your immediate area to collect dropped paper balls (ammo).
3. **Return to Spawn:** After 10 seconds, you must stop looting and immediately walk back to your team's spawn point (your team's bag).
4. **Respawn Countdown:** Once you touch the spawn point, you must count from 1 to 50 out loud and quickly.
5. **Re-entry:** After the count is finished, you are back in the game and can re-enter the field.

**Securing Virtues:** Players may only carry one Virtue Scroll at a time. To score for your team, you must successfully carry a scroll back to your team's base and place it on your Virtue Stack.

**Winning & Scoring:** At the end of the dedicated time (e.g., 15 minutes), the game ends. The Murabbi will count the number of Virtue Scrolls in each team's stack. The team with the most Virtue Scrolls wins the round. There is no point system; the highest count is the only metric for winning.

### Rules & Strategy

- **The Honesty Rule (Amanah):** This is the most important rule. Staying honest is key. Whenever you are hit, you must call it. If you are unsure if you were hit, call it anyway.
- **Ghost Looting:** Be strategic about where you get hit. If you are low on ammo, getting hit in an area with many dropped paper balls can be a great way to re-stock before you respawn. Be fair about counting your 10 seconds.
- **Spawn Camping:** Guarding an enemy team's spawn point to hit them as soon as they respawn is allowed as a valid strategy.

### Forbidden in the Game

- Physical contact (no pushing, grabbing, or tackling)
- Moving your team's base or spawn bag
- Carrying more than one Virtue Scroll at a time

### The Virtue Scrolls (Emaani Sifaat)

The folded papers are inscribed with the following qualities. Repetition is allowed to make enough for all the scrolls.

Sabr (Patience), Shukr (Gratitude), Tawakkul (Trust in Allah), Ikhlas (Sincerity), Taqwa (God-consciousness), Sidq (Truthfulness), Amanah (Trustworthiness), Adl (Justice), Ihsan (Excellence), Haya (Modesty), Shuja'at (Courage), Istiqamah (Steadfastness), Yaqeen (Certainty), Tawbah (Repentance), Afw (Forgiveness), Himmah (High Ambition), Jihad al-Nafs (Struggle against the self), Qana'ah (Contentment), Rahmah (Mercy), Ithar (Altruism), Firasah (Insight), Birr (Righteousness), Wafa (Loyalty/Fidelity), Hilm (Forbearance), Tadabbur (Contemplation).

## Activity 2: Breach & Clear

### Setup

- **Teams & Roles:** Divide players into two even teams. For the first round, designate one team as Attackers and the other as Defenders.
- **The Fortress:** Define the defensive area. A playground structure is ideal, but any clearly marked area with good cover will work. Defenders must stay within this area.
- **The Objective Bag:** The "flag" is a bag filled with papers inscribed with Emaani Sifaat (Virtue Scrolls). Have some chits with Emaani Sifaat written on them and don't tell Shabab. At the end of the game tell them what was in the bag, and explain the importance of these Sifaat. This bag is placed in a visible but defensible spot deep inside the Fortress.
- **Ammunition:** Each player on both teams starts with 10 paper balls wrapped in tape. To create these, crumble sheets of used A5 sized paper into balls and wrap transparent tape around them.
- **Attacker Spawn Point:** The Attackers' spawn point should be placed well out of shooting range of the Fortress, but not so far that it takes too long to get back into the action.
- **Timekeeper:** One Murabbi must be designated as the official timekeeper with a stopwatch.

### How to Play

The game is played in two rounds, with teams switching roles for the second round.

**Round 1:**

- **The Objective:** On the Murabbi's signal, the round begins and the timekeeper starts the clock.
- **Attackers' Goal:** Capture the objective bag and bring it back to your spawn point.
- **Defenders' Goal:** Prevent the Attackers from capturing the bag until the time limit (e.g., 8-10 minutes) expires.
- **Attacker Rules (Respawn Process):** Attackers have infinite respawns. When an Attacker is hit, they must follow the Core Gameplay Rules for being hit (see below). After the 10-second "Ghost" phase, they must walk back to their spawn point, count to 30 aloud, and then they can re-enter the battle.
- **Defender Rules (Respawn Process):** Defenders have 4 hits each. When a Defender is hit, they must follow the Core Gameplay Rules for being hit. On the 1st, 2nd, and 3rd hit: After the "Ghost" phase, they must go to a designated "recovery spot" inside the Fortress and count to 50 aloud before they can fight again. On the 4th hit: The Defender is eliminated and must sit out for the rest of the round.
- **End of the Round:** The round ends when either the Attackers capture the bag (the time is recorded) or the overall time limit is reached.

**Round 2: Switching Sides:** The teams now switch roles. The former Defenders become the new Attackers, and vice-versa. The clock is reset. The new Attacking team now attempts to capture the bag in the same scenario.

### Winning Conditions

This game is a time trial. The first team of Attackers sets the time to beat. To win, the second team of Attackers must capture the bag faster than the first team. If the first team failed to capture the bag within the time limit, the second team only needs to succeed in capturing it at all to win. If both teams fail to capture the bag, the game is a draw.

### Core Gameplay Rules

- **The Honesty Rule (Amanah):** This is the most important rule. Staying honest is key. Whenever you are hit by a paper ball, you must call it immediately. If you are unsure if you were hit, call it anyway.
- **The Ghost Looter Rule (Getting Hit):** Immediately raise a hand and loudly call "HIT!" If you are carrying the objective bag, you must drop it on the spot. For the next 10 seconds, you are a "ghost." You cannot be shot, shoot anyone, or touch the objective bag. However, you can move around your immediate area to collect dropped paper balls (ammo). After 10 seconds (be fair about counting), your ghost phase ends, and you must proceed to your designated spawn/recovery point.

### Forbidden in the Game

- Defenders leaving the designated Fortress area (unless a specific rule allows it)
- Attackers not returning to their spawn point after the ghost phase
- Throwing the objective bag (it must be carried)
- Ghosts interfering with active players (e.g., body blocking, giving directions)

### Tips for Strategic Play

**For Attackers:**

- *Coordinated Waves:* With a 30-second respawn timer, attacking one-by-one is a guaranteed failure. Group up at the spawn and rush the Fortress together to overwhelm the defenders.
- *Strategic Ghosting:* Use the Ghost Looter rule to your advantage. Try to get hit in areas where there is a lot of dropped ammo to resupply yourself and your returning teammates.

**For Defenders:**

- *Play Cautiously:* Your 50-second respawn timer is extremely punishing. Every hit takes you out of the fight for a long time. Use cover and play smart.
- *Target Priority:* The most important target is always the player carrying the objective bag. Coordinate your fire to stop them at all costs.
