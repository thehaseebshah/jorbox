### Infiltration

##### Game Objective
The goal of the Operatives is to successfully complete 3 out of 5 missions. The goal of the Spies is to cause 3 missions to fail. The Spies know who their fellow spies are, but the Operatives are in the dark, forced to rely on deduction and trust.

##### Players & Roles
- Total Players: 7 Shabab
- Operatives (4): Strive to complete missions successfully.
- Spies (3): Work quietly to fail missions.

Only the Murabbi knows the identity of the Spies.

#### Setup (Murabbi’s Tasks)
1. Prepare Materials: Role Slips (4 Operative, 3 Spies), Mission Cards (3 sets of SUCCESS & FAIL).
2. Assign Roles: Secretly hand one role slip to each player. Keep secret.
3. Spies Reveal: Close eyes, Spies open and see each other, close eyes, everyone open.
4. Explain the Rules: Describe game flow.

##### Game Flow
The game consists of 5 rounds, or "Missions." Each mission follows these steps:

##### 1. Appoint the Mission Ameer
- For the first mission, the Murabbi randomly assigns one player to be the Mission Ameer (leader).
- For all subsequent missions, the role of Ameer passes one person to the left (clockwise).

##### 2. Propose a Team
- The Ameer chooses exactly 3 players for the mission.
- The Ameer can choose any players, including themselves. After selecting, the Ameer clearly announces the proposed team to the group.

##### 3. Vote on the Team
- Every player (all 7) now votes on whether to approve the Ameer's proposed team.
- The vote is public and simultaneous. On the Murabbi's count, players vote by hand: Raise Hand (Approve), Lower Hand (Reject).
- If a majority (4 or more players) approves, the team goes on the mission.
- If the team is rejected, the role of Ameer passes to the next player, and the process repeats.
- Important: If 5 teams are rejected in a row for a single mission, the Spies team automatically win the entire game.

##### 4. Mission Execution
- The players on the approved team are each given two cards: one "SUCCESS" and one "FAIL" card.
- Each team member secretly chooses one card to play for the mission and gives it to the Murabbi face-down.
  - Operatives: Must play a SUCCESS card.
  - Spies: May play either a SUCCESS or a FAIL card.
- The Murabbi collects the cards, shuffles them to keep them anonymous, and then reveals them to everyone.
  - If all cards are SUCCESS, the mission passes.
  - If one or more cards are FAIL, the mission fails.

##### Victory Conditions
- The Operatives win if 3 missions pass.
- The Spies team wins if 3 missions fail (or if 5 consecutive teams are rejected by vote).

##### Conduct & Fair Play
- No one may lie about their role or their secret mission vote.
- Players may discuss, question, or stay silent—but must not make false claims about their identity or actions.
- The Murabbi ensures smooth play and resolves any disputes.

##### Strategy Tips
**For Operatives:**
- Track who consistently approves or rejects teams, especially when a Spies is likely on the team.
- Pay close attention to which players serve on failed missions. A failed mission with 3 players has at least one Spies.

**For Defenders/Spies:**
- Occasionally vote to approve "good" teams (teams without Spies) to avoid suspicion.
- Choose when to play a FAIL card carefully. Failing a mission too early can raise alarms.
