### Infiltration

#### Game Objective

The goal of the Operatives is to successfully complete 3 out of 5 missions. The goal of the Spies is to cause 3 missions to fail. The Spies know who their fellow saboteurs are, but the Operatives are in the dark, forced to rely on deduction and trust.

#### Players & Roles

- **Total Players:** 7 Shabab
- **Operatives (4):** Strive to complete missions successfully.
- **Spies (3):** Work quietly to fail missions.

Only the Murabbi knows the identity of the Spies.

#### Setup (Murabbi’s Tasks)

##### Prepare Materials

- **Role Slips:** Prepare 7 slips of paper: 4 labeled “Operative,” 3 labeled “Spy.”
- **Mission Cards:** Prepare 3 sets of mission cards. Each set should contain one "SUCCESS" card and one "FAIL" card.

##### Assign Roles

Secretly hand one role slip to each player. Players must keep their role secret.

##### Spies Reveal

Ask everyone to close their eyes.

Say, “All Spies, open your eyes and look at each other.” (Pause for a moment). “Now close your eyes.”

Say, “Everyone, open your eyes.”

##### Explain the Rules

Describe the game flow and answer any procedural questions. The game flow is described below.

#### Game Flow

The game consists of 5 rounds, or "Missions." Each mission follows these steps:

##### 1. Appoint the Mission Ameer

For the first mission, the Murabbi randomly assigns one player to be the Mission Ameer (leader).

For all subsequent missions, the role of Ameer passes one person to the left (clockwise).

##### 2. Propose a Team

The Ameer chooses exactly 3 players for the mission.

The Ameer can choose any players, including themselves. After selecting, the Ameer clearly announces the proposed team to the group.

##### 3. Vote on the Team

Every player (all 7) now votes on whether to approve the Ameer's proposed team.

The vote is public and simultaneous. On the Murabbi's count, players vote by hand:

- **Raise Hand:** Approve the team.
- **Lower Hand:** Reject the team.

If a majority (4 or more players) approves, the team goes on the mission. Proceed to Mission Execution.

If the team is rejected, the role of Ameer passes to the next player in clockwise order, and the process repeats from Step 2 (Propose a Team).

**Important:** If 5 teams are rejected in a row for a single mission, the Spy team automatically win the entire game.

##### 4. Mission Execution

The players on the approved team are each given two cards: one "SUCCESS" and one "FAIL" card.

Each team member secretly chooses one card to play for the mission and gives it to the Murabbi face-down.

- **Operative Members:** Must play a SUCCESS card.
- **Spy Members:** May play either a SUCCESS or a FAIL card.

The Murabbi collects the cards, shuffles them to keep them anonymous, and then reveals them to everyone.

If all cards are SUCCESS, the mission passes.

If one or more cards are FAIL, the mission fails.

The Murabbi should mark the outcome (Pass or Fail) on a public piece of paper. The role of Ameer then passes to the next player, and the next mission begins.

#### Victory Conditions

The Operatives win if 3 missions pass.

The Spy team wins if 3 missions fail.

(The Spy team also win instantly if 5 consecutive teams are rejected by vote).

#### Conduct & Fair Play

- No one may lie about their role or their secret mission vote.
- Players may discuss, question, or stay silent—but must not make false claims about their identity or actions.
- No hidden signals (e.g., notes, winks, text messages) are allowed.
- The Murabbi ensures smooth play and resolves any disputes.

#### Strategy Tips

##### For Operative Members:

- Track who consistently approves or rejects teams, especially when a Spy is likely on the team.
- Pay close attention to which players serve on failed missions. A failed mission with 3 players has at least one Spy.

##### For Spy Members:

- Occasionally vote to approve "good" teams (teams without Spy members) to avoid suspicion.
- Choose when to play a FAIL card carefully. Failing a mission too early can raise alarms.
