### Human Knot
#### How to Play
Watch [this video guide](https://www.canva.com/design/DAGrcbXXAQg/kXYsSv2Zvi15-W-3-MMQkQ/watch).

Ask questions to Shabab such as:

- What strategies did you use to untangle the knot?
- How did you communicate without speaking?
- What did you learn about teamwork and cooperation?

What are other learnings did they get from this activity:

- You will see that there will be a leader in your Shabab group who will be leading.
- That activity will be icebreaking for Shabab with the fun in activity enabling them to start developing relations with each other.
- Shabab will be able to learn how they can solve problems together in a team with cooperation.
