### Natural Direction Finding
#### How to Play
Navigation is a critical skill for outdoor adventures, survival situations, and fostering self-reliance. It enhances your confidence and reduces vulnerability to technological failures. Several natural methods can be used to find your direction.

##### 1. Celestial Navigation
This technique involves using celestial bodies like the sun, moon, and stars to find your way.

**Using the Sun:**
- *Solar Noon:* The sun is at its highest point in the sky at roughly solar noon, indicating South in the Northern Hemisphere and North in the Southern Hemisphere.
- *Shadow Method:* Place a stick upright in the ground and mark the tip of its shadow. After 15-30 minutes, mark the new position of the shadow's tip. A line drawn between these two marks points from west (the first mark) to east (the second mark).
- *Watch Method:* In the Northern Hemisphere, point the hour hand of an analog watch towards the sun. The line that bisects the angle between the hour hand and the 12 o'clock mark will point South.

**Using the Stars (at Night):**
- *Polaris (The North Star):* The North Star is a reliable indicator of North as it is positioned almost directly above the Earth's North Pole. *Finding Polaris:* Locate the Big Dipper constellation. Identify the two "Pointer Stars" that form the outer edge of the Dipper's bowl. Extend an imaginary line from these stars, and it will lead you directly to Polaris. The altitude of the North Star above the horizon corresponds to your latitude.
- *Using the Moon:* In the Northern Hemisphere, the illuminated side of a crescent moon points roughly towards the west (sunset direction).
- *Using Planets:* Bright planets like Venus and Jupiter, often visible after sunset or before sunrise, can help determine direction based on their position.

##### 2. Other Natural Methods
- *Natural Markers:* Moss often grows more abundantly on the north side of trees in the Northern Hemisphere.
- *Landmarks:* Use distinct features like mountains or rivers for orientation.
- *Animal Behaviour:* Some animals travel along established paths which can be used for direction.
- *Wind Patterns:* A prevailing wind that consistently blows from one direction can help with orientation.
