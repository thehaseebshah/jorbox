# Dil ki Sun

## How to Play
**Inspired by:** The Mind

- **Age Limit:** 14+
- **Group Size:** 5–10 players
- **Duration:** 30 minutes
- **Prep Time:** < 5 minutes
- **Difficulty:** 3
- **Code:** P-430

### Reference
Watch: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uXl8MC0GMYE

### Items Needed
- Role Cards (e.g., Mujahid/Jasoos)
- Mission Cards (Success/Fail)
- Voting Tokens (Approve/Reject)
- Can be handmade
