### Handball
#### Game Setup  
Teams:  
- 2 teams, each consisting of 6 players (or adjust based on your group size). You can have multiple groups playing if you have more than one football.  
Field:  
- Mark a rectangle (~20x10 meters) using chalk, cones, or sticks, or bags (backpacks)  
- Include:  
	- A halfway line.  
	- Two goal areas, each 2 to 3 meters wide, marked with cones or bags.  
	- Around each goal, mark a 3-step square "no-entry zone" (similar to a goalie box). Only the goalkeeper is allowed inside this zone.  
Ball:  
- Use any football or handball-sized ball. A ball smaller than a football would be preferred more, if you have one.  

#### How to Play  
- Objective:  
	- Score by throwing the ball into the opponent’s goal (between the cones).  
	- Each goal = 1 point.  
- Starting Play:  
	- A coin toss decides the starting team.  
	- The game begins from the halfway line.  
- Ball Movement:  
	- Players can dribble (bounce) the ball freely.  
	- You can take up to 3 steps while holding the ball, both before and after dribbling.  
- After 3 steps:  
	- You must pass, shoot, or dribble.  
- Goal Area Rule ("No-Entry Zone"):  
	- Around each goal is a 3-step square box (similar to a goalkeeper area).  
	- Only the goalkeeper can enter this zone.  
	- Shooters must throw from outside the box, no jumping inside or landing in it!  
- Defence Rules:  
	- Defenders can block shots and passes using their hands and body.  
	- No pushing, hitting, tripping, or aggressive contact.  
	- Light shoulder-to-shoulder contact is allowed.  
- Shooting Rules:  
	- Shots must be taken from outside the no-entry zone.  
	- The ball must be thrown below shoulder height when entering the goal area. A ball above shoulder height will not be a goal.  
- Time Rule:  
	- A player can hold the ball for up to 3 seconds without dribbling.  
- Out of Bounds:  
	- If the ball goes out, the opposing team restarts with a throw-in from that spot.  
- Game Time:  
	- Play 2 halves of 10 minutes each, with a short break in between.  
- Safety Tips  
	- Teach safe contact and smart defense.  
	- No shoving or wild tackles, it’s handball, not parkour wrestling.  
	- Rotate goalies often so everyone gets a chance.
