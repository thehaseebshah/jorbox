### Water Filtration
#### Instructions
This activity provides hands-on learning about the importance of clean water and how natural materials can be used to filter contaminants. It encourages critical thinking about the efficiency of different filtration methods.

##### Instructions for Building a Filter
- **Introduction:** Begin with a discussion on the importance of clean water for humans and ecosystems. Highlight how this is a good survival skill.
- **Setup the Funnel:** Cut the bottom off a plastic bottle to create a funnel. Place this funnel upside down into the top of a second, intact bottle which will collect the clean water.
- **Layer the Filtration Materials:**
  1. First, place a coffee filter or a piece of fine cloth at the neck of the funnel bottle to prevent the filtering materials from falling through.
  2. Assemble the layers inside the funnel in the following order from bottom to top:
     - Gravel or small stones (to filter out larger debris).
     - A layer of sand (for finer particles).
     - A layer of activated charcoal (for absorbing impurities).
     - A final layer of sand.

##### Video Instructions
*(You don’t have to use 3 bottles, you can do this with just one bottle – and use tissue instead of cloth)*

##### The Filtering Process
Slowly pour the dirty water into the top of the filtration system. Observe how the water drips through the layers and collects in the bottom bottle.

##### Discussion
After filtering, discuss the visible changes in the water's clarity and the significance of water purification. Reflect on how to contribute to water conservation and pollution prevention.
