# Doley Sholey

## How to Play
**Inspired by:** Muscle Power

- **Age Limit:** 14+
- **Group Size:** 5–10 players
- **Duration:** 30 minutes
- **Prep Time:** < 5 minutes
- **Difficulty:** 4

### Reference
Watch: [Excalidraw Whiteboard - 29 August 2025](https://www.loom.com/share/e091416f7dd6423fa195470ff48b9d68)
