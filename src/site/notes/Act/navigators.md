### Navigators
#### How to Play
Watch: https://www.loom.com/share/6688c94113aa4a1194d4c3fc6e9ffb9e

##### Important Note
Always have one Murabbi accompanying each group to make sure they don't cheat. Keep swapping the handicap mechanisms every 5 minutes – so the person with blindfolds will become for example the person who can't walk, after 5 minutes. Always have a Murabbi guarding the location of the guides.

Whenever a shabab breaks the handicap rule, intentionally or by mistake, freeze the group for 10 seconds.
