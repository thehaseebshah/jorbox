### How to Play
Note: Mashwarat process will be followed before the start of each activity/challenge wherever required. Appointment of Ameer for every challenge is a must.

In Islam, meetings and gatherings are an important aspect of community, communication, and decision-making. There are certain principles and etiquettes that Muslims generally follow when conducting meetings. Here is a 10 step process & guidelines that you will explain to shababs in detail:

> [!note] 10-Step Islamic Mashawrat Framework
> 1. **Intention (Niyyah):** Begin with a sincere intention for *"ALLAH KI RAZAA"*, whether seeking knowledge, making a decision, or solving a problem.
> 2. **Invocation of Blessings (Dua):** Start with a supplication seeking guidance and wisdom from Allah to set a positive tone.
> 3. **Greeting (Salam):** Begin with *"Assalamu Alaikum"*. Our Prophet S.A.W always started his conversations with Salam to promote unity and respect.
> 4. **Orderly Conduct & Roles:** Appoint an Ameer before the start. Assign one person for meeting notes/conclusions and one person for **Zikr** to invoke Allah's blessings and keep Satan away.
> 5. **Consultation (Shura):** Seek suggestions actively. Even the Prophet S.A.W, the wisest of mankind, consulted his companions and often adopted their suggestions.
> 6. **Moderation and Balance:** Avoid overly lengthy discussions that lead to fatigue or waste time.
> 7. **Respect for Time:** Meetings should start and end on time out of respect for participants' schedules.
> 8. **Decision-Making:** Align decisions with the Quran and Sunnah. Once decided, place firm trust (*Tawakkul*) in Allah.
> 9. **Conclusion with Dua:** Conclude with supplication asking for guidance, acceptance, and forgiveness.
> 10. **Gratitude:** Express gratitude to Allah for the opportunity to gather and engage productively.
