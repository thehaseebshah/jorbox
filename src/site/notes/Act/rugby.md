### Rugby
#### Game Setup  
Teams:  
- 2 teams, each consisting of 6 or more players.  
Field:  
- Use chalk, sticks, or tape to mark:  
- A halfway line (center of the field).      
- Two scoring lines (one at each end of the field).  
- Two cones or bags on each end to mark the scoring zones.  
Ball: 
- Use any football (no need for a rugby ball).  

#### How to Play  
- Objective:  
	- The goal is to get the ball past the opponent’s scoring line and place it on the ground in the scoring zone to earn 5 points.  
- Starting Play:  
	- A coin toss decides the starting team.  
	- The game begins from the halfway line.  
- Moving the Ball:  
	- Players can run with the ball or pass it backward/sideways only (no forward passes).  
	- Kicking is optional, but it should be safe and low. (You can try two variations – one with kicking allowed and one with not allowed)  
- Tackling:  
	- A tackle occurs when a player grabs the player with the ball to stop their movement or gently bring them to the ground.  
	- Multiple players can tackle together.  
	- The tackled player must immediately drop the ball.  
- After a Tackle:  
	- Any player from the opposing team can pick up the ball and continue play.  
- Time Limit Rule:  
	- A player can hold the ball for a maximum of 5 seconds before passing.  
- Out of Bounds:  
	- If the ball goes out of bounds, restart with a throw-in from the point where the ball went out.  
- Game Time:  
	- The game consists of two halves, each lasting 10 minutes, with a short break in between.  
- Scoring  
	- To score 5 points, place the ball on the ground in the opponent's scoring zone  
- Safety First  
	- Teach safe, controlled tackling (start gently).  
	- No rough play.  
	- Emphasize teamwork, passing, and fun.
