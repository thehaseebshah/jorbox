### Chala Kooch
#### How to Play
There are two teams and one of them on their turn is given something to hide. And the team secretly hides that thing in one of the hands. So if there are five players, there are 10 hands. The other team tries to guess one by one which hand is empty and At last find out which hand has the secret item.
