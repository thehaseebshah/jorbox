# Pakran Ghumai

## How to Play
**Inspired by:** Triangle Tag

- **Age Limit:** 14+
- **Group Size:** 5–10 players
- **Duration:** 30 minutes
- **Prep Time:** < 5 minutes
- **Difficulty:** 5
- **Code:** O-110

### Reference
Watch: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a_2epGmCpwg
