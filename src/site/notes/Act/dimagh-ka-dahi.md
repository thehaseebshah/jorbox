### Dimagh Ka Dahi
#### How to Play
Watch: [02-03 Dimagh ka Dahi](https://www.canva.com/design/DAGrdy2qMPA/zzP6uqKMUh7S9sHyX1TxUA/watch?utlId=h53a89b7e74)  

Make groups of 6 from the Shabab, each group will have two Teams, Team A and Team B. Each group will be guided by a Murabbi. Create similar cards out of paper as shown in the video. Include words that align with Nazriati themes. You can use ChatGPT to generate the words and the banned words. You don’t need a buzzer, just point out the rule violation by interrupting the clue-giver. You can use a smartphone timer for keeping time.  

Variation:  
Watch [this](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4QeA4nrcQV0) video carefully.  

For both variations you can take inspiration from, or use directly the Main Deck and the Charades (Banned Words) deck in the [JorBox App](http://thehaseebshah.github.io/jorbox-app)
