# Piddi Piddi with Bats

## How to Play
- **Age Limit:** 14+
- **Group Size:** 5–10 players
- **Duration:** 30 minutes
- **Prep Time:** < 5 minutes
- **Difficulty:** 4
- **Code:** C-120

### Reference
Watch: https://www.loom.com/share/f09e80b47fc04d5ca398ae17b3c48980

- Mission Cards (Success/Fail)
- Voting Tokens (Approve/Reject)
