# GupShup

## How to Play
- **Age Limit:** 14+
- **Group Size:** 5–10 players
- **Duration:** 30 minutes
- **Prep Time:** < 5 minutes
- **Difficulty:** 5
- **Code:** O-150

### How to Play
Ask these questions to groups to start a conversation.
