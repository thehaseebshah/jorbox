### Rummikub
#### How to Play
*Note: Content not retrieved from the Canva API due to repeated failures. Only metadata is available.*

Rummikub is a classic tile-based game. The standard ruleset is widely available online:

- Players take turns placing tiles from their rack into sets of runs (consecutive numbers in the same color) or groups (same number in different colors).
- The first player to place all their tiles on the table wins.
- Each player must "open" by placing tiles worth at least 30 points before adding to existing sets.
