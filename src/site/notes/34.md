# 4 on a Bench

## Learning Objectives

Sharpen memory and focus, build teamwork and cooperation, practice clear communication under pressure.

## Activity 1: 4 on a Couch

Watch this video: [Four on a Couch | Actually Fun Youth Games](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=)

Use a bench in the park or a dedicated area in the park as the "Couch".
