# Dimagh Ka Dahi

## How to Play
**Inspired by:** Monikers, Banned Words, Poetry for Neanderthals

- **Age Limit:** 14+
- **Group Size:** 5–10 players
- **Duration:** 30 minutes
- **Prep Time:** < 5 minutes
- **Difficulty:** 5
- **Items Needed:** Word Cards

### Reference

Watch: [02-03 Dimagh ka Dahi](https://www.canva.com/design/DAGrdy2qMPA/zzP6uqKMUh7S9sHyX1TxUA/watch?utlId=h53a89b7e74)

**Variation:** Watch [this](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4QeA4nrcQV0) video carefully.

### Variation (Group-based)

- Make groups of 6 from the Shabab, each group will have two Teams — Team A and Team B.
- Each group will be guided by a Murabbi.
- Create similar cards out of paper as shown in the video.
- Include words that align with Nazriati themes.
- You can use ChatGPT to generate the words and the banned words.
- You don't need a buzzer, just point out the rule violation by interrupting the clue-giver.
- You can use a smartphone timer for keeping time.

For both variations you can take inspiration from, or use directly, the **Main Deck** and the **Charades (Banned Words) deck** in the [JorBox App](http://thehaseebshah.github.io/jorbox-app).
