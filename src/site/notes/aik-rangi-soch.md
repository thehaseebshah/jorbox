# Aik Rangi Soch

## How to Play

**Inspired by:** Wavelength (with drawing and non-related drawing prompts — last two like non-sensory)

- **Age Limit:** 14+
- **Group Size:** 5–10 players
- **Duration:** 30 minutes
- **Prep Time:** < 5 minutes
- **Difficulty:** 5
- **Code:** P-020

### Items Needed

- Role Cards (e.g., Mujahid/Jasoos)
- Mission Cards (Success/Fail)
- Voting Tokens (Approve/Reject)
- Can be handmade
