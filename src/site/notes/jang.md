# Jang

> [!info] Quick Overview
> - **Age Limit:** 12+
> - **Group Size:** 3+
> - **Duration:** 60 minutes
> - **Prep Time:** 60 minutes
> - **Difficulty:** 5 / 5
> - **Items Needed:** Map, tokens, paper, pencil
>
> *Reference: Kriegsspiel Framework*

> [!quote] Welcome to the Commander's Tent
> The real fun of **Jang** isn't combat minutia — it's making big strategic decisions: *"How am I going to get to the battle? Where am I committing my troops? Where is my reserve? What's my plan to get out?"*

## 1. The Three Key Roles: Who's Who on the Battlefield

> [!note] Player Roles
> - **The Umpire:** Game referee, storyteller, and engine. Acts as AI for subordinate commanders and keeps the game moving smoothly.
> - **The Blue Player:** Leading the forces of the "Blue" army.
> - **The Red Player:** Leading the opposing forces of the "Red" army.

## 2. Your First Game's Toolkit: What You'll Need

> [!tip] Three Sets of Three
> - **People:** 1 Umpire + 2 Players (or groups led by an Ameer).
> - **Tables:** 3 separate tables (Umpire, Blue, Red).
> - **Maps:** 3 identical maps (master view for Umpire, individual fog-of-war maps for players).
> - **Pieces:** 3 identical token sets (6–12 pieces per player).

> [!warning] A "Double Blind" Setup & Fog of War
> Each player's table only shows their own unit positions. The Umpire's master map shows everything. This creates the true **Fog of War** — uncertainty and incomplete battlefield information.

## 3. Setting the Stage: Your Mission

> [!important] Historical Battles & Scenarios
> - **Create a "McGuffin":** A central conflicting objective (a vital bridge, crossroads, or fortress).
> - **Historical Inspiration:** Draw scenarios from Muslim commanders like *Khalid ibn al-Walid at Yarmuk*, *Saladin at Hattin*, *Tariq ibn Ziyad at Guadalete*, or *Baybars on the Nile*.
> - **Winnable, Not Balanced:** Asymmetrical scenarios make for fascinating strategy, but must be winnable for both sides.

## 4. The Rhythm of the Game: The Umpire's Cadence

The core gameplay loop is a four-step framework: **Report → Orders → Movement → Combat Resolution**.
