# Social Deduction

> [!info] Learning Objectives
> Strategic thinking · Observational skills (*Muraqabah*) · Deductive reasoning · Team-based problem-solving · Concise & subtle communication · Adab of discussion · Upholding truthfulness (*Sidq*) under pressure · Vocabulary building in Islamic & local contexts · Decolonized perspective.

## Activity 1A: Spy Club (for Juniors)

> [!note] Setup & Logistics
> - **Players:** 4–15 Shabab per group.
> - **Topic Pages:** 10 topic pages featuring a 4x4 grid of 16 related words.
> - **Murabbi Role:** Game moderator, selects topic page & secret word, and writes it on a slip.

### How to Play

1. **Secret Word & Spy Selection:** Murabbi selects one Spy secretly (shown a blank slip) while showing the secret word to all other players.
2. **Questioning Round (5 Mins):** Players take turns asking one indirect, subtle question. Everyone (including the Spy) must answer truthfully without revealing the exact word. No duplicate target questions allowed.
3. **Discussion Round (2 Mins):** Players debate who they suspect the Spy is. The Spy acts natural.
4. **Voting Round:** Players simultaneously point to suspect on Murabbi count.

> [!success] Winning Conditions & Scoring
> - **Spy Wins (1 pt):** If not identified by majority vote, OR if identified but correctly guesses the secret word.
> - **Non-Spies Win (1 pt):** If they correctly identify the Spy AND the Spy fails to guess the secret word.

> [!warning] Forbidden in Spy Club
> - Lying (Even the Spy must not lie)
> - Directly mentioning the secret word
> - Giving obvious hints or breaking seriousness through jokes

> [!tip] Strategy Tips
> - **For the Spy:** Listen carefully to subtle clues to deduce the word.
> - **For Non-Spies:** Ask broad questions to expose inconsistencies, but keep clues indirect so the Spy doesn't guess it!

## Activity 1B: Infiltration (for Seniors)

> [!note] Role Distribution (7 Players)
> - **4 Operatives:** Complete 3 out of 5 missions.
> - **3 Spies:** Cause 3 missions to fail quietly. Spies know each other; Operatives are in the dark.

> [!important] Game Flow
> 1. **Appoint Mission Ameer:** Rotates clockwise each round.
> 2. **Propose Team:** Ameer selects 3 players for the mission.
> 3. **Public Vote:** All 7 vote on the team. 4+ votes approve. If 5 proposed teams are rejected in a row, Spies win automatically!
> 4. **Mission Execution:** Approved team submits secret "SUCCESS" or "FAIL" cards. Operatives MUST play SUCCESS; Spies may play SUCCESS or FAIL. 1+ FAIL card fails the mission.

> [!tip] Strategy Tips
> - **For Operatives:** Track voting patterns and watch who served on failed missions.
> - **For Spies:** Blend in by occasionally approving clean teams. Time your FAIL cards wisely!
